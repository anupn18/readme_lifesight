## Redefining Marketing Measurements for the Privacy-First Era

Lifesight was founded on the ethos that marketers need holistic, trusted, and accurate measurement, in order to improve marketing outcomes and make the best decisions for their business. 

The world of marketing analytics is in the midst of a profound transformation and evolution. As the landscape continues to evolve, we believe it's more important than ever to protect consumer privacy while still enabling marketers to achieve their objectives effectively and efficiently. We are passionate about helping marketers navigate this changing landscape, providing them with tools that are effective, adaptable, and respectful of user privacy.

We believe that measurement has to be done by an independent and unbiased solution. We are a team of engineers, marketers, data science and product minds that have been obsessed with keeping up with understanding and measuring marketing effectiveness with the latest in AI technology. We have worked with hundreds of brands of all sizes to help them navigate the privacy-first era and weaning them off conventional attribution.

### Problems & Challenges in the Industry

Before we get into why we exist, let’s first address a few things with regards to problems that have plagued the marketing industry over the past decade or so, and also largely gone unaddressed. Marketing (and its measurement) has been broken long before the privacy-first era got here.

#### Marketing is measured in silos

From the advent of marketing, measurement has been fragmented with publishers creating their own “walled garden”. Online vs offline, in-platform reporting, post campaign analytics etc. How does one truly understand how the combined marketing efforts are impacting the KPI?

#### Conventional attribution is not grounded incrementality & causality

Marketers spend billions of dollars in advertising because the adtech giants say it's working. Let’s not forget the true function of marketing, which is to drive incremental outcomes (and not take credit for outcomes that were coming anyway)

#### Marketers predominantly rely on publishers grading their own homework

Publishers and media owners have been self-reporting and self-attributing for far too long and have managed to earn complete distrust but yet have so much control over marketers that it’s gone unquestioned.

#### Decision-making being driven by vanity metrics

Marketers have been trained to chase efficiency metrics like CPC, CPM, CPA and ROAS causing their focus to be on improving metrics that don’t affect the bottom line. It is common to see marketing teams exceed their “targets” and get incentives while business profits don’t move at all.

#### Marketing teams measure outcomes separately

Most marketing teams (brand, performance, lifecycle etc) work towards different goals (typically their own vanity metrics) like audience exposure, brand equity, conversions etc. however none of that measures what is driving incremental outcomes to the business KPIs. 

#### Marketing and finance teams are not on the same page

Marketing leads are usually questioned about their targets, what strategies they will use and which metrics will “prove” their marketing work. The problem is that the metrics the finance team is targeting (profit) is not the same as the marketing team (ROAS).

#### Fraud and misreporting is still here and ignored

Since the advent of digital advertising, fraud and misreporting has been around. It is a known fact that the industry as a whole completely ignores this fact and very little is done in the industry to tackle it because it helps marketers get “better” results and everyone shrugs their shoulders when something is misreported by the publisher.

#### Ever-changing environments

Privacy regulations/technology, new media channels, macroeconomic conditions & consumer behaviors, the world is more erratic than ever. The environments that marketers are forced to operate in is constantly changing making it nearly impossible to plan and act with confidence.

## Navigating the privacy-first era

Today’s marketers are facing a dual challenge: preserving user privacy while still being able to measure and optimize their marketing campaigns effectively. The rise in strict privacy regulations and tech limitations is making it increasingly difficult to use traditional, user-level and identifier-based measurements. These changes are causing seismic shifts in how marketers operate and will cause severe disruptions.

- Ad waste is only going to get much worse if new measurement approaches are not adopted.
- The ability to reach the target audience is diminished significantly
- The cost of acquiring customers is going to keep rising with adtech giants having the upper 1P data and ability to charge their “tax”
- More devices and browsers will continue to implement privacy technology
- Privacy enhancing tech like clean rooms and sandboxes are temporary workarounds to “identify” users and only works with complex partnerships.
- Existing tools like GA4, in-platform reporting, MTA platforms are only going to diminish in quality further.

## Why we built Lifesight

It is our mission to democratize causal AI-powered marketing measurement tools for non-technical marketers and teams to leverage with ease. We believe every business deserves the ability to make better decisions, with the same technology used by giants in their category, so they stand a chance to compete with them.

When we set out to build this new platform, we looked at the gaps that remain in the market (along with measurement inaccuracies) and clear patterns started to emerge. This new breed of measurement platform has to do more than just measure marketing. 

- Most businesses don’t have access to data engineers, data scientists and analytics experts.
- Marketers mainly had access to marketing and sales, with near zero visibility into other business data
- Marketers and analysts struggle with data wrangling, transformations, analysis etc.
- Traditional marketing mix modeling was outdated, slow and expensive
- In-platform incrementality testing was inaccurate and inconsistent
- Machine learning tools and complete business data was only accessible to data scientists
- Most AI/ML projects fail within the organization and are almost never utilized for decision making.

Of course things were different at large digital native brands who have everything the average business doesn’t. 

## So this is what we built for marketers

#### Connect

This serves the data engineer and analyst in the team and is going to help connect all their data sources, automatically transform it and explore it. Marketers can integrate data with 1-click integrations where available and instantly pull and refresh data without worry.

#### Measure

This serves the data or marketing scientist in the team and is going to help run unified measurement models and incrementality tests without code. Marketers get access to world class marketing mix modeling, attribution modeling and incrementality testing frameworks that can be implemented with a few clicks.

#### Action

This serves the brand marketer, performance marketer and media planner and is going to help use the insights generated by the models to make strategic and tactical decisions. Marketers can build and forecast scenarios to optimize budgets and campaigns while continuously improving their models.

#### Analyze

This serves the executive teams and is going to help them track the performance of marketing contributions towards the goal and make further decisions based on that. The CEO, CFO and CMO can track the same metric to scale their business with incrementality intelligence and work together to grow the business profitably and sustainably.
