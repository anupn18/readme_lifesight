Lifesight is an agentic unified marketing measurement platform that gives you the exact incremental value of every marketing channel so you can forecast, plan, and grow profitably. We help marketing, finance, and growth teams move past biased, platform-reported metrics and make decisions backed by causal proof, not opinions.

Instead of guessing what's working, Lifesight triangulates the truth using three complementary methodologies, Causal Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM), Incrementality Testing (Geo-Based Testing), and Causal Attribution. Lifesight then layers AI agents on top to turn that truth into action to produce forecasts, budget recommendations, and enable one-click execution back to your ad platforms.

# Why "Agentic"?

Most measurement tools stop at a dashboard and leave you to interpret it. Lifesight's AI agents read your causal model directly, answer your marketing questions in plain-English with confidence intervals attached, and can push decisions back into your workflow so the gap between asking a question and acting on the answer shrinks from hours or days to just minutes.

## The Decision Loop

Lifesight is built around one continuous loop that gets sharper every cycle:

- **Observe** - Every channel, online and offline, unified into a single honest view. Agents clean and centralize your data automatically.
- **Orient** - Unified measurement (MMM, incrementality, and attribution) corrects the biased picture platform-reported metrics give you.
- **Decide** - Ask a question in plain English. Agents recommend the best move, backed by evidence and confidence.
- **Act** - Push decisions to your ad platforms in one click, or let agents execute within your guardrails.<br />Learn - Every test recalibrates the model, so your next decision is better than the last.

The faster you can run this loop, the faster you can grow.

## Core Methodologies

Lifesight's measurement rests on three methodologies, used together rather than in isolation.

## Causal MMM

A macro-level statistical model that quantifies each channel's contribution to revenue and other KPIs, adjusted for seasonality, pricing, and other external factors. Used for long-range budget planning and channel strategy.

## **Incrementality Testing (Geo-Based Testing)**

Controlled geo-experiments that isolate the causal, incremental lift of a channel or campaign by comparing test and control markets. This is the ground truth that calibrates every other model in the platform.

## **Causal Attribution**

Incrementality-adjusted, journey-level attribution that shows which touchpoints actually drove a conversion, rather than which touchpoints simply appeared in the path.

Together, these three methodologies create a single, de-biased view of marketing effectiveness, what Lifesight calls Unified Measurement.

Measurement alone doesn't move a business forward, so Lifesight pairs it with:

- **Planning & Forecasting** — Forecast profit and growth before you spend, and plan around seasonality and promotions.
- **Spend Optimization** — Automated, constraint-aware recommendations that reallocate budget toward incremental profit, not just efficiency metrics.
- **AI Agents** — Ask Lifesight plain-English questions and get answers, recommendations, and board-ready reporting, generated automatically.
- **Creative Intelligence** — Measure every creative's incremental impact, not just its engagement metrics.<br />Analyze — Dashboards and reporting built to surface causal insight, not just raw numbers.
