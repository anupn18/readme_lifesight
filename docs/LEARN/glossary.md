Marketing measurement comes with its own vocabulary, and small differences in meaning lead to big differences in decisions.&#x20;

Reported ROAS and incremental ROAS can point you to opposite channels. A lift test and a holdout sound alike but answer different questions. When your team, your agency and your finance partners use these terms the same way, you read results correctly, spot when a number is misleading, and make budget calls you can defend in front of leadership.

The Lifesight Glossary gives you a clear, practical explanation of every key measurement term, so you spend less time decoding reports and more time acting on them. Each entry walks you through:

- **Definition:** What the term means, in plain language
- **Example:** How it shows up in a real marketing scenario
- **Why it matters:** The decisions it helps you make
- **What influences it:** The factors that move the metric up or down
- **How to improve it:** Practical levers you can pull
- **How it connects:** Its relationship with other metrics, so you see the full picture rather than one number in isolation

To build a shared measurement language across your team, explore the full list of terms.
