Measurement Academy is Lifesight's free, self-paced learning program that helps you find out which of your marketing investments actually drive growth, and how to prove it. You learn how incrementality testing, Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) and calibrated attribution work together, so your budget decisions rest on causal evidence rather than what each ads platform reports.

Every course ends with a certification you can add to your LinkedIn profile, so you can show current and future employers that you can lead modern measurement. The lessons are video-based, available 24/7, and open to both new and experienced marketers with no prerequisites.

## Courses that take you from fundamentals to hands-on practice<br />

### 1. Foundations: Know what's really driving your growth (Free, available now)

Master the fundamentals of modern marketing measurement across 8 modules and 43 lessons, in about 5 hours. You earn your certification on completion.

1. **Why Marketing Measurement Is Broken:** The problem every marketer faces but few can articulate clearly.
2. **Incrementality, The Gold Standard:** The only way to know what's actually working.
3. **Marketing Mix Modeling Demystified:** Understanding the strategic engine of modern measurement.
4. **Calibrated Attribution:** Making daily decisions with causal confidence.
5. **Unified Marketing Measurement:** Why one methodology isn't enough, and how they work together.
6. **AI-Powered Measurement:** How AI transforms every step of the measurement workflow.
7. **From Measurement to Decision Intelligence:** How measurement powers smarter decisions and profitable growth.
8. **Your Measurement Journey:** Putting it all together, and what to do next.

To begin the course, enroll <Anchor target="_blank" href="https://academy.lifesight.io/courses/foundations/">here.</Anchor>
