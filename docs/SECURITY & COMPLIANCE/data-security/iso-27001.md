Lifesight is ISO 27001 certified, affirming our commitment to maintaining the highest standards of information security. This globally recognized certification ensures that we have established a robust Information Security Management System (ISMS) that protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of sensitive data. ISO 27001 compliance demonstrates that Lifesight systematically manages risks related to information security and takes proactive steps to safeguard data against threats. 

This certification enhances trust with our clients, ensuring that their data is handled securely and in compliance with international standards, further solidifying Lifesight’s position as a trusted and reliable data partner.
