The **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** ensures the protection and privacy of personal data for individuals within the European Union. Lifesight is fully committed to complying with GDPR by following its core principles, which include lawfulness, transparency, data minimization, and purpose limitation.

We respect the rights of individuals, providing them with access to their data, the ability to rectify errors, request deletion (the right to be forgotten), restrict processing, and object to certain activities. Lifesight ensures data security with strict measures like encryption and consent management while partnering only with third parties that meet GDPR standards.

For more information about our practices, compliance, and rights of individuals, visit our full GDPR documentation [here](https://www.lifesight.io/gdpr).
