Lifesight is SOC 2 compliant, demonstrating our commitment to maintaining the highest standards of security, confidentiality, and privacy for our clients' data. This certification ensures that we have implemented robust internal controls to safeguard data across our systems and processes. By adhering to SOC 2 requirements, Lifesight guarantees that customer data is managed securely and is protected from unauthorized access or breaches. Our SOC 2 compliance not only strengthens trust with our clients but also ensures that our platform meets industry-recognized best practices for data protection and privacy, making us a reliable partner for businesses handling sensitive information.
