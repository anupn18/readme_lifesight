Please follow the instructions carefully to ensure a smooth and efficient onboarding process.

## **Step 1: Add A Record to Your DNS Settings**

To begin, you need to add an A record to your DNS settings. This will enable the whitelabeling of your agency’s domain.

### **A Record Information:**

* **Host:** @ (or your domain root)
* **Type:** A
* **Value:** `35.208.92.49`

## **Step 2: Provide Required Information - Agency Logo, Sign Up Page Image, Privacy Policy and Terms of Service Documents**

We need some specific information from your agency to complete the onboarding process. Please gather and send us the following:

### 1. Agency Logo

* Logo to be used on the platform

### **2. Custom Image for Sign-In Page:**

* Dimensions: 720x1048 pixels
* Include your agency logo and any other elements you want to display.

### 3. **Privacy Policy:**

* A document outlining your agency’s privacy practices.
* This should reference Lifesight as an enabler.

### **4. Terms of Service:**

* A document detailing the terms under which your services are provided.
* This should also reference Lifesight as an enabler.

### 5. **Support Email:**

* An email address that will be used to send support-related communications to onboarded brands.

## **Step 3: Whitelabeling and Sign-Up Pages**

### **Sign-In Page Whitelabeling:**

* Lifesight will whitelabel your sign-in page. This means that the sign-in interface will be customized to reflect your agency’s branding.
* URL Format: `{agency_domain}/login`

## **Step 4: Privacy Policy and Terms of Service**

* Ensure your Privacy Policy and Terms of Service clearly reference Lifesight as an enabler.
* These documents are essential for compliance and transparency.
* These documents will be added on the platform as part of the white labeled solution.

## **Step 6: Automated Emails**

Lifesight will send automated emails for the following scenarios:

### 1. **New Customer Invites:**

When a new customer is invited to join the platform.

### 2. **Password Reset:**

When a user requests a password reset.

### 3. **New Sign-Ups:**

When a new user signs up through the designated sign-up page.

These emails ensure timely communication and smooth onboarding for your customers.
