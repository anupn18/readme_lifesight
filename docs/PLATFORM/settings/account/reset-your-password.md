Follow these steps to set a new password in case of a forgotten password.

1. Go to the `Login` page.
2. Click on `Forgot Password`.
3. Now, enter your registered Email Address to reset your password and click on the `Send password reset link`.
4. Check your registered email inbox entered in the last step. This is where you would receive an email with a link to set up a new password.
5. Click on the link in the email, enter the new password and click `Save`. 
6. You can now log into your account with the new password.
