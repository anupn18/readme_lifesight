The Account section allows users to view and manage their personal account information and security settings. This section is essential for maintaining accurate account details and ensuring the security of your workspace access.

<br />

## View Account Details and Change Password

In this section, users can review their account information, including name, email, and contact details. Users can also change their password here to improve account security. It is recommended to update your password periodically to protect against unauthorized access. [View account details and change password](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/view-account-details-and-change-password)

## Reset Your Password

If you've forgotten your password or need to reset it for security reasons, this section provides a step-by-step guide to recovering your account. Users will receive an email with instructions to securely reset their password and regain access to their workspace. [Resetting your password](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/reset-your-password)
