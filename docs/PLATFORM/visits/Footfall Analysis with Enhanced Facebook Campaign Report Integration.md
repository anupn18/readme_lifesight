In the evolving landscape of digital advertising, Lifesight Footfall Measurement enriches digital advertising analytics by integrating Facebook reports into its platform. This feature streamlines ad campaign tracking, consolidating various reporting tools into one user-friendly interface. By simplifying the process, Lifesight facilitates a more efficient and effective monitoring and analysis of ad campaigns, leveraging Facebook's

extensive data within a cohesive system.

## Simplified Integration and Reporting

1. Setup Integration

   * Access the Lifesight platform and navigate to the 'Integration' section.
   * Select 'Facebook Reporting' and assign a name to your report.  
     ​

     ![](../images/0fbda413fd1dc3cd29c7f29799a0f19d.png)

     ​
2. Link Facebook Account

   * Follow the instructions to log into your Facebook account, linking it with Lifesight for a seamless data flow.
3. Activate Facebook Reports

   * When setting up a new measurement campaign in the 'Measurements' section, opt to enable Facebook reporting on Lifesight platform. Please click on "Enable Reporting"

     ​

     ![](../images/535260efc5d40896b1c682e5e8bc8ce3.png)

     ​
4. Campaign selection and synchronization

   * Choose the desired ad account, which will automatically import all campaign IDs from your selected account into Lifesight platform.
   * From the imported campaign ID list, select the specific campaigns you want analyze, and add the campaign date range for tracking.  
     ​
5. Accessing and Analyzing Campaign Data

   * A 'Facebook Reporting' tab will appear within the 'Measurements' section.
   * This tab provides a comprehensive overview of your campaigns, offering detailed insights and analytics derived from the integrated data.
