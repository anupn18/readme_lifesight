You can make manual adjustments to the budgets of your campaigns or ad sets directly within the Optimizer's **Campaigns** tab. These changes are treated as one-time interventions, allowing you to fine-tune spending based on immediate market needs or specific campaign knowledge.

* For **Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO)** campaigns, you can edit the budget at the campaign level.
* For **Ad Set Budget Optimization (ABO)** campaigns, you can edit the budget at the ad set level.

### How to Make a Manual Budget Change

To manually adjust a budget, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the **Optimizer** module from the left-hand menu.
2. Ensure you are on the **Campaigns** tab.
3. In the **Channel Breakdown** table, locate the campaign or ad set you wish to modify.
4. Click the **pencil icon** next to the `Recommended Budget` or `Bid Strategy` values.
5. Enter your new desired budget or bid amount in the editable field and confirm the change.

> 🚧 **Warning: Overriding Automated Recommendations**
>
> Making a manual change to a campaign's or ad set's budget will override the automated changes recommended by the Optimizer. This action is recorded as a manual intervention, and the platform will prioritize your manual setting over its own recommendation for that specific item.

### Tracking Changes in the Logs Tab

All manual interventions are recorded for full transparency and historical tracking. To review any manual changes you have made, check the Logger tab.
