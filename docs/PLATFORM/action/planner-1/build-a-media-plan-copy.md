The Planner module is a powerful forecasting tool within the Lifesight UMM Platform. It allows you to create data-driven budget plans and predict revenue outcomes based on the insights generated by your **Marketing Mix Models (MMM)**. By simulating different budget allocations, the Planner helps you understand how to best invest your marketing spend to maximize revenue and ROAS.

<br />

### The Planner Homepage

<br />

When you navigate to the Planner, you will see the homepage, which lists all of the budget plans that have been previously created and saved in your workspace.

<br />

This view allows you to quickly find and revisit past analyses. The list contains the following information:

* **Name:** The custom name given to the plan.
  * **Model Name:** The specific Marketing Mix Model that was used as the basis for the plan's forecasts.
    * **Scenarios:** The number of different budget scenarios contained within the plan.
      * **Forecast Period(s):** The timeframes being forecasted across the scenarios (e.g., Month, Quarter, Six Months).
        * **Created On:** The date the plan was first created.
          <br />
          To begin, click the **+ Create Plan** button in the top-right corner.
          <br />

### Generating a Revenue Scenario (Step-by-Step)

<br />

A "Plan" serves as a container for one or more "Scenarios." Each scenario represents a unique simulation based on a specific budget, timeframe, and set of constraints.

<br />

#### Step 1: Initial Plan Setup

<br />

First, give your overall plan a descriptive name (e.g., "Q4 2025 Growth Plan"). This will help you identify it later on the homepage.

<br />

#### Step 2: Configuring a Scenario

<br />

Next, you'll configure your first scenario within the plan.

<br />

**Select Model:** Choose the Marketing Mix Model you want to use for the forecast from the dropdown menu. The planner will use this model's historical learnings to make its predictions.

<br />

**Set Forecast Period:** Define the timeframe for the simulation by selecting a "Forecast for" period (e.g., Quarter) and the specific "Optimized based on" date range. This basis period tells the model which historical conditions are most similar to your intended forecast period.

<br />

#### Step 3: Setting Goals and Budget Constraints

<br />

This step is crucial for defining the boundaries of your simulation.

<br />

**Enter Target Budget:** Input the total budget you want the planner to allocate across your channels.

<br />

**Apply Budget Constraints:** Constraints provide intelligent guardrails for the simulation. The logic applied depends on the status of your channels:

<br />

> ℹ️ **How Constraints Are Applied**> - **For Causally Calibrated Channels:** When you select a constraint level (Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive), the system automatically sets appropriate lower and upper spending limits for each calibrated channel. The simulation then optimizes spend within these smart boundaries.
> > - **For Non-Causally Calibrated Channels:** To ensure predictions are grounded in historical data, the system applies a ±10% change to the target budget during simulation for these channels.
>
>
>
> > 💡 **Tip: Achieve Tighter Control with Causal Calibration**> To gain more precise control over your budget simulations, we recommend running geo-experiments to causally calibrate your channels. This provides the model with more robust data for setting its optimization guardrails.
> >
> >

#### Step 4: Running the Simulation

<br />

Once you are satisfied with the configuration, click the **Run Scenario** button to initiate the simulation.

<br />

### Editing, Adding, and Comparing Scenarios

<br />

A single plan can house multiple scenarios, allowing you to compare different strategies.

* **Editing:** Before saving the plan, you can always go back and edit your scenario's configuration and re-run the simulation.
  * **Adding Scenarios:** Click the **Add Scenario** button to create a new, parallel scenario. For example, you could create a "Conservative" scenario and an "Aggressive" scenario to compare the potential revenue outcomes of different risk levels.
    <br />

### Saving a Plan

<br />

Once you have generated and analyzed your scenarios and are satisfied with the insights, click the **Save Plan** button in the top-right corner. This will save the entire plan, including all of its scenarios, to the Planner Homepage for future reference.
