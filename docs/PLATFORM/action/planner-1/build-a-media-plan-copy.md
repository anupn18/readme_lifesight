The next step after creating a MMM model is to create a plan.

The Planner module allows marketers to simulate various scenarios, run simulations, and analyze potential outcomes to achieve their goals. It leverages models to forecast future performance with predictions available for one month or one quarter based on the selected base period.

## Steps for Creating a Plan

1. Navigate to the Action module, and click on the Planner.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/8880af9d6a19e10beaf92598ffc357e6071d10a66527661ab4525848f224e705-plann.jpg" />

1. Click on the "Create Plan" button and give your plan a descriptive name.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/6d2c7559489f94148210a044f01ad0de7dfacd6390accfc328ee19202ef35085-create.jpg" />

2. Name your plan on the top-left. Choose the MMM model you want to use to build your plan.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/e1dcce9ee45a2788e55a7a5c4902f9093fe10c1e8e8ffc62c777efe4b3754a3e-mm.jpg" />

3. Once you choose a MMM model the Scenario pane loads. 

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/3c41bca4d5924b9f00b6fa1868a4d01cf4665f0b41f901a06c548b23a7bb169b-sc.jpg" />

4. **Enter a Goal** — Choose between Budget, ROAS, or optimize for KPI as a plan goal.
5. **Choose Forecast for period** — Choose whether you want to forecast for a month or a quarter.
6. **Optimize based on** — Based on your forecast, select the base period. For example, if your model is built until May 2024 and you're forecasting for June 2024, select either May 2024 or June 2023 as the base period. For a quarter forecast, if you're planning for June 2024 - August 2024, select either March 2024 - May 2024 or June 2023 - August 2023. Learn more on how to [select the best base period]() here
7. **Input Target Budget:** Enter your planned spend for the target period. For example, if you have a planned spend for June 2024, input it as your Target Budget.
8. **Constraints:** [Constraints](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/compare-plans) help define how much the model can control and adjust the budget to achieve the Target KPI set in the plan. There are 5 constraints available:
   1. **Current** - This allows the model increase/decrease media spends from 95-105% of your current media budget.
   2. **Conservative** - This allows the model increase/decrease media spends from 75-150% of your current media budget.
   3. **Moderate** - This allows the model increase/decrease media spends from 50-200% of your current media budget.
   4. **Aggressive** - This allows the model increase/decrease media spends from 1-499% of your current media budget.
   5. **Manual** - If you have planned spends for all channels, select Manual as a constraint and provide the upper and lower bounds for spends across all channels or tactics.
9. Click the `Run Scenario` button at the bottom of the page. After a few seconds, your plan will be created and you can view insights and forecast on the right-hand side of the page.

### Viewing Insights and Outputs

After successfully creating a plan, you'll be able to view the forecasted values, graphs, optimized budget, recommendations, and a worksheet within the Planner module.

Click the `Fullscreen` icon from the Forecast pane to expand and view the insights in fullscreen.

> 🚧 Click the Minimize button to return to the Planner configuration page when in fullscreen mode. Clicking on the "Back" button will result in exiting the Planner and losing your progress.

## Create & Compare multiple plans

Lifesight allows the creation of multiple scenarios within a single plan. You can create and name up to 5 scenarios, each representing a different marketing strategy, and compare their insights by selecting them in the dropdown menu.

Learn more about [comparing plans](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/compare-plans)

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/84035459f0daa03efb677f2f368029ec3ad4dae026571f38e79b02921ded491b-addd.jpg" />

> 📘 Comparing scenarios
>
> After clicking the Fullscreen icon, you can add compare your baseline with any 2 scenarios you created and view insights. View a apples to apples comparison of how different scenarios perform:
>
> * **Compare forecasted KPI** - View a trend of your KPI over time for different scenarios.
> * **Compare Current VS Optimized Budget** — Media investments, Incremental KPI, Incremental ROAS/CPA
> * **Media Allocation Recommendations** - View current, recommended spend, their corresponding current and incremental KPI and ROAS/CPA in a table format. You can download your Recommendations in a CSV format to share with your team.
> * **Budget Worksheet** - View platform wise average daily media spend and weeky budget pacing for each platform. You can download your budget worksheet in a CSV format to distribute to your team.

## Saving and Downloading Plans

To save your plan, click the "Save Plan" button. 

> 🚧 Note that once a plan is saved, you cannot make changes to its scenarios.

By following these steps and referring to the provided images, you can effectively use the Planner module to create, manage, and analyze your marketing plans.
