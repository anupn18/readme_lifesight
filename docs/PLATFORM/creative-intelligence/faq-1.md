**Why are my creatives compared only within the same channel and asset type?**

Performance depends heavily on context. A video and a text ad earn clicks in very different ways, and the same is true across channels. Comparing only like with like keeps every score and signal fair, so the read reflects the creative rather than the format.

**Why is one of my creatives not classified as a Winner or Loser yet?**

Winners and Losers only include creatives with enough spend and enough days live to judge confidently. A creative with very little data stays out of those states until there is enough evidence, which protects you from acting on noise.

**A creative's numbers still look good, so why is it flagged as fatiguing?**

Fatigue is based on the trend in click-through rate, not a single snapshot. If CTR has been sliding over recent days, the creative is flagged early so you can refresh it before results drop further, even while the headline numbers still look healthy.

**What does the score number actually mean?**

Both the click score and the conversion score are percentile ranks from 0 to 100, measured against creatives of the same asset type. A score of 80 means the creative performs better than 80% of comparable creatives.

**Why does each creative show only one signal?**

The signal is the single most important thing about the creative right now. Showing one prioritized read keeps the decision clear, so you know the first thing to act on rather than sorting through a list.

**What is a "Zombie" creative?**

A Zombie is no longer new but is barely being shown while still spending. It usually points to a delivery issue, so it is worth reviewing the setup or pausing it.

**My account tracks leads, not revenue. Will the scores still work?**

Yes. Set your primary KPI to CPA, and the headline number and the spend-related signals adjust accordingly. Everything else, including the click and conversion scores, states, and fatigue, works the same way.

**What should I do first when I open the page?**

Start with the four states. Scale your Winners, refresh anything Fatiguing, pause your Losers and Budget drains, then watch your New creatives and Early winners for the next round.
