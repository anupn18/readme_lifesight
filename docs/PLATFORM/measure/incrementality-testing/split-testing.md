Split Testing utilizes a sample of the total audience to create treatment and control groups to evaluate the impact of marketing. The split groups are synced as audiences to advertising channels where campaigns and advertising tactics must be set up using the synced audiences. Multiple Test (Treatment) Groups can be specified for multiple advertising channels.

<br />

## What is Audience Split Testing?

Audience split testing is an advanced technique used in Incrementality Experiments to measure the incremental value (e.g., revenue, orders, store visits) generated by marketing campaigns. It works by dividing a brand’s audience into two groups: the control group and the treatment group. The campaign targets only the treatment group, while the control group is excluded. The key metric is the 'lift,' which compares performance outcomes (e.g., revenue) between the two groups, revealing the true channel effectiveness.

**The idea is to measure the impact of campaigns and ad tactics for the treatment groups against the control group to measure if the campaign or ad tactic had an incremental impact on a specified KPI/metric.**

## Key Components in Split Tests:

* Control Group: The group exposed to the original version (baseline) to measure against.
* Test Group: The group that experiences the new variation to assess its effectiveness.

## Benefits of Split Testing:

* Data-Driven Optimization: Make decisions based on concrete performance metrics rather than guesswork.
* Performance Insights: Identify which variations improve user experience or drive more conversions.
* Reduced Risk: Test new ideas on a subset of your audience before rolling them out to everyone.
* Maximized ROI: Focus resources on strategies proven to work, improving overall marketing effectiveness.
