Target Benchmarks are a powerful for marketers who know a Target KPI and want to optimize their efforts to achieve them. Lifesight lets users input their marketing KPI goals and get automated recommendations at a granular level in the Channel breakdown table.

View powerful insights such as Scale-up, Stop, and Reduce spends across platforms, tactics, campaigns, ad groups, and at an ad-level granularity.

## How to use Target Benchmark

1. Select the `Target ROAS` button from the top-right of the Attribution dashboard.
