Connecting your Shopify store with Lifesight after you sign up is super easy. All you need is admin access to your Shopify store and your Shopify store ID. 😃

1. Copy your Store ID from Shopify's admin panel. In this case, it's Pupman.

![](https://files.readme.io/45ec7f2472353b1d90ac91302b95c9b20a3612e05416c6a7237da7f178234286-image.png)

<br />

2. Next, enter your Shopify store ID in the field below and click Connect.

![](https://files.readme.io/2045698276e444b9737b152e9b94d0198213518fd122d533be57fda83cffe798-image.png)

<br />

3. After that, you'll be asked to log in to your Shopify store and install our app. If you're already logged in, the installation process will start automatically.

![](https://files.readme.io/16e317623087d12d1a831eb9b7b74c39733ffb4b6cd544e3e6008e672f3a3c03-image.png)

<br />

## Congratulations! You've now successfully integrated your Shopify store with Lifesight. That's 3 small steps from you, and one giant leap for your DTC brand.

Alternatively, you can also [install the Lifesight app](https://apps.shopify.com/lifesight) directly from the Shopify app store.
