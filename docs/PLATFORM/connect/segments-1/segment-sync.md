The Segment Sync feature enables you to seamlessly sync user segments from Lifesight's platform to your preferred marketing and advertising platforms. By leveraging Lifesight’s robust data capabilities, you can automate the process of keeping your audiences up to date across multiple channels, ensuring your marketing efforts are always reaching the right people. This integration provides a streamlined workflow for enhancing customer engagement, improving targeting accuracy, and driving marketing performance.

## Steps to sync segments to ad platforms

1. Navigate to Connect > Segments.
2. Select the "Options" icon from a chosen segment and click on "Sync Settings"

5. The Audience will be synced to the audiences section in the selected ad platform to run retargeting campaigns.
