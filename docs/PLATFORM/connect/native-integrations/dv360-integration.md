## Steps to integrate with DV360

1. Go to Connect > Integrations page from the navigation bar on the left.
2. Search for "Google DV360" and click on the tile.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/e0b8022f93251b2aa9ee109603efb9aa3e50862d93ccaf2e5d016089f3f5259d-Integrations_-_Apps_Option_1.png" />

3. Follow the OAuth process to grant necessary permissions to Lifesight.
4. The Google DV360 tile status changes to "Active"
