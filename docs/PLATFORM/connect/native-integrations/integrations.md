Integrations are the foundation of any robust Unified Marketing Measurement (UMM) strategy. By connecting your various marketing, sales, and data platforms to Lifesight, you create a single, comprehensive data stream that powers accurate, cross-channel insights.

> [!NOTE]
> **Built for Security and Reliability**
> All of Lifesight's native integrations are built and maintained in-house by our engineering team. This approach ensures maximum security, data integrity, and seamless compatibility with our UMM platform, eliminating the risks associated with third-party connectors.

### How Integrated Data Powers Your Measurement

Connecting your sources is the first step toward unlocking the full potential of the Lifesight platform. Data pulled from your integrations is used to:

* **Build Marketing Mix Models (MMMs)**: Use granular cost, impression, and conversion data from every channel to model their true impact on your business KPIs.
* **Conduct Incrementality Tests**: Measure the causal uplift of your marketing campaigns by isolating variables and comparing them against a comprehensive baseline of your marketing activities.
* **Optimize Campaigns**: Feed performance data into our optimization engines to receive actionable recommendations on budget allocation and campaign strategy.
* **Generate Unified Reports**: Consolidate performance metrics from dozens of platforms into a single, intuitive dashboard to uncover the complete story of your marketing effectiveness.

### Supported Integration Categories

Lifesight supports a wide range of platforms across your entire growth stack. Click on a category to explore the specific integrations available.

* **Social Ad Platforms**: Connect platforms like Meta (Facebook & Instagram), TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, and more.
* **Search Ad Platforms**: Integrate with Google Ads, Microsoft Advertising, and other search marketing channels.
* **Programmatic Platforms**: Pull in data from Demand-Side Platforms (DSPs) like Google Display & Video 360, The Trade Desk, and others.
* **Connected TV (CTV) Platforms**: Measure the impact of your CTV advertising from providers like Roku, Hulu, and more.
* **Data Platforms**: Sync your data warehouses and business intelligence tools, such as Google BigQuery, Snowflake, and Amazon Redshift.
* **E-commerce Platforms**: Integrate your online store data from platforms like Shopify, Magento, and WooCommerce.
* **Marketing Automation Platforms**: Connect CRM and messaging platforms like Braze, Iterable, and Hubspot.
* **Affiliate Marketing Platforms**: Track performance from affiliate networks like Impact, PartnerStack, and CJ Affiliate.
* **Finance Platforms**: Incorporate financial data from systems like Stripe and Chargebee for a complete view of revenue.
* **Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Tools**: Sync lead and customer data from CRMs like Salesforce and HubSpot.
* **Tracking and Analytics Platforms**: Bring in behavioral data from tools like Google Analytics and AppsFlyer.
* **Custom Connectors**: Use our flexible connectors to upload data from any internal or proprietary source via CSV or cloud storage.

### Security and Data Integrity

We understand that your data is your most valuable asset. Our commitment to data security is at the core of our integration philosophy.

> [!WARNING]
> Your data is always encrypted in transit and at rest. We use secure, industry-standard API protocols to connect to your sources, and access is strictly limited to the permissions you grant during the authentication process. We never store raw credentials on our platform.

### Requesting a New Integration

If you don't see a tool you use in our integration library, our team can likely build it for you.

To request a new integration, please contact our support team at `support@lifesight.io` or reach out to your Customer Success Manager with the following details:

* The name of the platform.
* A link to their API documentation (if available).
* The primary business use case you aim to solve.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**1. What if I don't see the specific platform I use listed?**
Please reach out to us by following the steps in the "Requesting a New Integration" section above. We are constantly expanding our library and prioritize new builds based on customer feedback.

**2. How often is data synced from my connected sources?**
Data is typically synced once every 24 hours. However, the exact sync frequency can vary depending on the specific API limitations of the source platform.

**3. Can I connect a custom data source, like my company's internal database?**
Yes. You can use our **Custom Connectors** to upload data via CSV file, SFTP, or from a cloud storage service like Amazon S3 or Google Cloud Storage. This allows you to incorporate data from any internal or proprietary system into your measurement models.
