## Overview

Google Analytics provides comprehensive insights into your website and app performance by tracking user behavior, acquisition channels, and conversions. By integrating with platforms like Google Ads, you can monitor the effectiveness of your ad campaigns, create audience segments based on real-time user data, and leverage these insights to refine marketing strategies. With Lifesight's integration, you can further analyze Google Analytics data to optimize audience targeting, push custom segments back into Google Ads, and measure the impact of your marketing efforts across multiple channels.

## Integrating Google Analytics on your workspace

1. Navigate to the Integrations tab in the left-hand menu bar.
2. In the search field, type "Google Analytics" to locate the integration.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/8f749cb7b016c9c5273b87beb08e29b93bbe33c8aaba339b7084fb53a3b782b8-Screenshot_2024-09-23_at_12.35.52_PM.png" />

3. Click on the Google Analytics tile and click on the "Connect to Google" button to begin the integration process.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/b4732a3d149538d46268c93269ea4a967c6f992e5db4f41d41302e8af6f9fed9-Screenshot_2024-09-23_at_12.36.01_PM.png" />

4. You will be redirected to log into your Google Analytics account and allow access to certain scopes.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/0290a11dab088eb8aa446bff4a81abb4110379922b94b44b59f89a36262f160c-Screenshot_2024-09-23_at_12.37.19_PM.png" />

5. Once you have successfully logged in, the status of the integration will change to ‘partial’ on the Integration page.
6. Click on the Google Ads tile and select an ad account you want to use with the workspace.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/73c9a4aedac9887f74ef94992ef452e34ffc9a1d79e1390da235534e50391d5e-Screenshot_2024-09-23_at_12.38.38_PM.png" />

7. The status will get changed to ‘Active’ and the ad data from the designated account will be available in the Attribution dashboard for unified reporting.
