Whether you're new to marketing measurement or transitioning from another tool, this section provides step-by-step instructions to help you set up your account, configure key settings, and get familiar with the platform's features. Our goal is to ensure a smooth and efficient onboarding experience so you can quickly leverage the platform to its full potential.

With detailed walkthroughs, best practices, and helpful tips, the Onboarding section is designed to support users of all experience levels. From setting up integrations to customizing your preferences, this guide will cover everything you need to know to get up and running in no time.
