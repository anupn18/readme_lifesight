Get a free trial here

## Steps to get started

1. Sign up for a free trial or request a demo
2. Our team will understand your goals, learn more about your existing strategy and qualify feasibility over a call.
3. Once you are ready, we will set up your dedicated workspace based your [data localization](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/data-localization-security-privacy-compliance) preferences.
4. You will be invited to your workspace through an email invite link
5. Click on the link to accept the invite and set your password and complete sign up
6. Login to your account
