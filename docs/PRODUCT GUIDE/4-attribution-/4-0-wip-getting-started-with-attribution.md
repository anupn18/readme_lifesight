Before you can act on causal results, your workspace needs three things in place: connected advertising data, promoted measurement inputs, and an active scenario to measure against.

The five steps below take you through each one in order, from connecting your platforms to opening Attribution and reviewing your first results. Work through them once, and your workspace is ready to show what is driving growth and whether spend is on track.

## 1.Connect Your Platforms So Every Dollar of Spend Is Accounted For

Your results are only as complete as the data behind them. Go to Integrations and connect each advertising platform your workspace uses.

Once connected, confirm that every connection is active and receiving current campaign data. A paused or stale connection leaves spend out of your totals, which shifts both your iROAS and your pacing against plan. Checking this now means the numbers you review later reflect your full media investment.

## 2. Promote the Models and Experiments That Make Your Results Incremental

This step is what separates causal results from platform-reported credit.

Create and validate the Marketing Mix Models and experiments you want to use for incremental measurement. Then promote the assets that should contribute to Attribution and model calibration (the process of tuning your models with experiment evidence so they stay accurate over time).

Only promoted assets inform your results, so promote the ones you trust most. The stronger your measurement inputs, the more reliable your incremental revenue, incrementality factor, and recommendations will be.

## 3. Set the Plan You Want to Measure Performance Against

Pacing and recommendations need a benchmark to work from. Prepare the planning scenario that provides your planned spend and the benchmark used by causal recommendations.

The Attribution Overview then compares your actual activity against this active scenario, so you can see at a glance whether you are on pace, running ahead of plan, or leaving budget unspent. Without an active scenario, you can still review performance, but pacing and Scale, Maintain, and Reduce recommendations will not have a plan to measure against.

## 4. Open Attribution and Confirm You're Looking at Current Data

Go to **Attribution** and select the date range you want to analyze.

Before you interpret anything, check the last updated indicator at the top of the page. This tells you when your data was last refreshed, so you know whether recent campaign changes are reflected in what you're seeing.

## 5. Start With the Big Picture, Then Drill Into the Detail

Use the Overview for a quick read on headline spend, incremental revenue, iROAS, pacing against plan, and which channels are contributing the most real growth.

When a result raises a question, move to the Breakdown to inspect performance at channel, tactic (the strategy or ad type within a channel, such as prospecting or retargeting), campaign, and ad set level. This is where you find the specific activity behind a channel-level number and decide exactly what to adjust.

## Best practices

A few habits will keep your results accurate and your decisions sound:

- Keep platform integrations active and current so no spend is missing from your totals.
- Use comparable date ranges when reviewing changes so you are measuring real shifts, not seasonal ones.
- Review platform-reported and incremental metrics together to see where platforms are over-crediting themselves.
- Confirm the active scenario before acting on pacing recommendations so every adjustment is measured against the right plan.
- Validate important changes with MMM and experiment evidence before making significant budget moves.

**Not seeing any data yet?** If your workspace shows no attribution data, check that your data pipeline is running, your measurement assets are promoted, and your permissions are configured correctly. Once these are in place, your results will begin to appear.
