Lifesight is a **agentic unified marketing measurement (UMM) platform**. It tells you how much of your revenue your media actually drove, and then helps you act on it.&#x20;

The suite turns causal evidence into decisions you can plan, execute, and track end to end, so the incrementality read, the budget you commit to, and the change you push to Google or Meta all come from the same model.

Platform-reported performance cannot do this. Self-attributed platforms grade their own homework, and once you add up the walled gardens plus last-click, the reported numbers typically account for around 140% of your revenue.&#x20;

Everything above 100% is double-counted conversions that would have happened anyway. Lifesight measures incremental contribution rather than claimed conversions, delivers the read inside the week, and closes the gap between the insight and the budget change.

### How the platform works

<br />**One engine, three causal methods**<br /><br />Every number in the product comes from a single causal engine that triangulates three methods and lets each one calibrate the other two.

| Method                 | Where it is strongest                                                                     | Granularity                  |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Causal MMM             | Portfolio-level direction across every channel, including offline and hard-to-track media | Channel and campaign, weekly |
| Geo experiments        | A clean, measured causal read on one hypothesis at a time, using geo holdouts             | Channel or tactic, per test  |
| Calibrated attribution | Daily, campaign and creative-level granularity, corrected for over-claiming               | Campaign and creative, daily |

The methods are connected rather than run in parallel. Measured lift from a geo holdout enters the next MMM run as an informative prior, so the model tightens with every test. Attribution is then calibrated against both, which keeps daily reporting consistent with the portfolio-level read instead of drifting from it.<br /><br />**One decision loop**<br /><br />The four products sit on five steps that repeat every cycle.

1. **Observe:&#x20;**&#x45;very channel, online and offline, in one view.
2. **Orient:&#x20;**&#x4D;MM, incrementality, and calibrated attribution correct the picture.
3. **Decide:&#x20;**&#x41;sk in plain English. Agents recommend, backed by the causal evidence.
4. **Act:&#x20;**&#x50;ush changes to the ad platforms in one click, inside your guardrails.<br />Learn. Every experiment calibrates the model for the next decision.

Agents draft, you approve. Every action is logged, attributed to a decision, and reversible.

### Measure: see what your spend actually caused


Measure establishes the causal baseline everything else is built on. It decomposes revenue into baseline and media contribution, then shows where the next dollar works hardest.

**Decide:&#x20;**&#x77;hich channels to scale and which to cap, and which hypotheses to test next.

**You get:**

- Contribution decomposition: baseline vs media, with carryover and adstock accounted for<br />Response curves per channel, with saturation points and diminishing returns made explicit
- mROAS and iROAS, alongside blended ROAS, so marginal and average performance are never confused
- Incremental CPA and incremental revenue per channel and tactic
- Confidence intervals on every estimate, so the strength of the evidence travels with the number
- Executive read-outs built for the meeting, not the dashboard
- Online and offline in one model: retail media, CTV, OOH, and in-store measured alongside paid search, paid social, affiliate, and email

**Who it's for:&#x20;**&#x43;MO and VP Growth, CFO and FP\&A, Performance and Media, Analytics.

**Worth knowing:&#x20;**&#x72;etail media networks are measured independently. A network reporting on its own value has the same self-attribution problem as any other walled garden.

To go deeper, see the Measure overview.

Forecast: commit to a plan you can defend<br /><br />Forecast turns the causal read into risk-aware scenarios and a profit-aligned plan. You commit to a range with the drivers stated, not a single point estimate.

**Decide:** which posture to run (Efficiency, Balanced, or Growth), how to pace spend by channel and week, and what triggers a re-plan.

**You get:**

- p50, p10, and p90 outcomes for every scenario, so downside and upside are budgeted for
- Sensitivity drivers ranking which assumptions move the forecast most
- Channel by week pacing with guardrails, caps, and floors attached
- Optimal allocation against your objective, constrained by saturation rather than by last year's split
- Re-plan triggers defined up front, so you know what evidence would change the plan

**Who it's for:&#x20;**&#x43;MO and VP Growth, CFO and FP\&A, Channel Owners, Analytics.

To go deeper, see the Forecast overview.

### Optimize: act on the plan inside your guardrails


Optimize is where the decision and the execution stop being two different jobs. You set the corridor, agents draft moves inside it, and approved changes write back to the ad platforms.

**Decide:&#x20;**&#x62;udget shifts, caps and floors, ramp rates, frequency and concentration limits.

**You get:**

- Ranked Scale, Cap, and Shift recommendations, each tied to a marginal response curve and a measured lift.
- Creative decisions as a decision band: maintain, refresh, or pause, read against weeks in flight and fatigue signals.
- Alerts when a channel breaches a guardrail, saturates, or drifts off pacing.
- A change log recording every action, the approver, and the decision behind it.
- Exports and write-back to Google, Meta, Amazon, and TikTok.

**Who it's for:&#x20;**&#x50;erformance and Media, Growth, Analytics, Finance reviewers.

**Worth knowing:&#x20;**&#x74;he guardrails are the feature. You define spend limits, channel rules, and approval thresholds first. Agents work inside that corridor, and every change is reversible.

To go deeper, see the Optimize overview.

Analyze gives finance, marketing, and agency partners the same reconciled view, so reviews stop being an argument about whose numbers are right.

### How to read what the platform tells you

Some expectations worth setting before your first read.

- **You will not get one perfect number:&#x20;**&#x54;hree methods produce three views, and where they diverge, the divergence is information. Analyze surfaces the delta rather than hiding it.
- **Accuracy is demonstrated, not published:** Instead of a headline accuracy score, models are graded out-of-sample through holdout backtests you can inspect.
- **A causal read takes weeks, not days:&#x20;**&#x43;onnectors are fast. Statistically sound incrementality is not.
- **Revisions come from a person:** If new evidence changes a past recommendation, you are told, rather than finding a silently updated chart.

The causal graph behind the model, meaning the encoded assumptions about what drives what, is inspectable at any time. It is not a black box.
