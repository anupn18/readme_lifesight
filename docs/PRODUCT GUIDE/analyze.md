The Analyze section provides powerful tools to evaluate the effectiveness of your marketing efforts, helping you track goals, measure user behavior over time, and gain valuable insights from cohort analysis.

## Goals

The Goals feature allows you to set and track specific marketing objectives. Whether it’s increasing conversions, improving retention, or achieving a particular ROI, this tool helps you monitor progress and determine whether your campaigns are meeting business targets. It also enables you to adjust strategies in real-time based on the performance against these goals.

## Cohorts

Cohorts are groups of users who share common characteristics or behaviors over a specific period. This feature allows you to analyze how different cohorts of users interact with your product or service, offering insights into retention patterns, lifetime value, and engagement levels. By tracking cohorts over time, you can understand customer behavior trends and make data-driven decisions to enhance the user experience.
