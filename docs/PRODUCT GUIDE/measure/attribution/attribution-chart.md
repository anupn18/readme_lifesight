## Metrics

This dropdown field lets users, choose any Attribution metric across any of these filters:

1. **Platform** - Choose from a list of connected Attribution channels to display your selected metric.
2. **Tactic** - Select any tactic from a list of connected Attribution channels to display your selected tactics.
3. **Campaign** - Choose any 1 or multiple campaigns from all your connected Attribution channels to view your selected metric
4. **Ad Groups** - Select 1 or multiple ad groups from all your connected Attribution channels to view your selected metric
5. **Ads** - Choose any 1 or multiple ads from all your connected Attribution channels to view your selected metric

## Compare to

Users can choose any metric, time period, attribution model or attribution window to compare to.

1. **Different metric** - Choose any other metric like Revenue or NC Revenue across any channel or sub-campaign granularity from across your connected Attribution channels. 
2. **Same metric different time** - Choose a different time period, either previous period or same period from a different year to compare.
3. **Same metric different model** - Select another Attribution model (Last Touch, Algorithmic, etc) to compare metrics.
4. **Same metric different window** - Select a different Attribution window to compare a metric against.

### Additional options

1. **Align axis** - Align chart axes for accurate, scale-to-scale comparisons, ensuring more precise and meaningful data analysis.
2. **Line/Bar graph** - Easily switch between line and bar charts for a fresh perspective on your data, helping you quickly spot trends and insights.
3. **Export** - Export your charts and data in formats like PNG, JPG, PDF, SVG, CSV, and XLSX for seamless integration into presentations and reports.
