# Test Hypotheses Generation

Start a Lifesight 4.0 Geo Experiment with a clear, decision-oriented hypothesis. The Goal step provides common hypotheses and also lets you write your own.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Hypothesis cards in the Goal step]

## Available hypothesis patterns

- Is this channel actually working?
- Should we spend more on this channel?
- What is the right spend level?
- Is our new tactic or strategy better?
- Which channel performs better?
- What happens with no paid ads?
- Is our MMM recommendation right?
- Which creative works better?
- Who should we target?
- Does this promotion or offer lift results?

## Write a strong hypothesis

A useful hypothesis identifies:

- The marketing action being changed
- The expected incremental outcome
- The KPI used to evaluate the result
- The markets and period in scope
- The decision that will follow the result

Example:

`Increasing Meta prospecting spend in selected markets will produce positive incremental revenue at or above the target iROAS during the test period.`

## Select a treatment

Choose **Hold-out** to pause activity in test markets or **Scale-up** to increase activity. Lifesight limits treatment choices when a hypothesis supports only one valid design.

[VIDEO PLACEHOLDER: Selecting a hypothesis and treatment]

## Connect the hypothesis to design

Use the Data and Design steps to confirm that the selected KPI, pre-treatment history, market granularity, cells, duration, and power analysis can answer the hypothesis.

> A hypothesis should be defined before reviewing results. Avoid rewriting it after seeing the measured outcome.
