# Attribution

Attribution in Lifesight 4.0 combines incremental measurement with active media plans so teams can understand what is driving business outcomes and how spend is pacing against the selected scenario.

The workspace is designed for causal decision-making. Incremental results are informed by promoted Marketing Mix Models and experiment evidence rather than touch-based credit rules.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Attribution workspace with Overview and Breakdown tabs]

## What you can do

- Compare platform-reported revenue with incremental revenue
- Review total spend, iROAS, incrementality factor, and net spend versus plan
- Monitor pacing against an active scenario
- Analyze performance from channel to tactic, campaign, and ad set
- Review Scale, Maintain, and Reduce recommendations
- Deploy a recommendation into the Decisions workflow

## Overview

The **Overview** tab summarizes performance for the selected date range. It includes headline metrics, pacing against plan, platform-reported versus incremental revenue, and channel performance ranked by incremental value.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Attribution Overview with headline metrics and pacing]

## Breakdown

The **Breakdown** tab provides detailed performance at channel, tactic, campaign, and ad set level. Search entities, choose columns, sort metrics, select rows to drill deeper, and open weekly spend details.

[VIDEO PLACEHOLDER: Moving from the Attribution Overview to a campaign breakdown]

## Target scenario

The active target scenario determines the benchmark and planned spend used for pacing and recommendations. Select the scenario control to review the available metrics, benchmark value, and analysis periods.

> Causal recommendations depend on the measurement and scenario data available in your workspace.
