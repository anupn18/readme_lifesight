# Conversions

Lifesight 4.0 includes conversion metrics in the Attribution Breakdown rather than a separate Conversions tab.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Conversion columns in the Attribution Breakdown]

## Available conversion metrics

Depending on the connected platform and available measurement data, you can review:

- Platform Conversions
- Incremental Conversions
- Platform CPA
- Incremental CPA
- Incrementality Factor

Platform Conversions are reported by the advertising platform. Incremental Conversions estimate the conversions causally driven by advertising.

## Review conversion performance

1. Go to **Measure > Attribution**.
2. Open the **Breakdown** tab.
3. Select **Choose columns**.
4. Add the conversion and CPA metrics required for your analysis.
5. Compare results across channels, tactics, campaigns, or ad sets.

[VIDEO PLACEHOLDER: Adding conversion columns and drilling into campaigns]

## Interpret CPA

Platform CPA uses platform-reported conversions:

`Actual Spend / Platform Conversions`

Incremental CPA uses causally estimated conversions:

`Actual Spend / Incremental Conversions`

Use incremental metrics when making causal budget decisions. Use platform metrics to understand what the advertising platform reports and to diagnose differences.

> Conversion columns appear only when the selected range and source data include the required values.
