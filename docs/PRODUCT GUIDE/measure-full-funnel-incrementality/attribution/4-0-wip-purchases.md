# Purchases

Lifesight 4.0 does not include the legacy Purchases tab with separate Orders, Products, and Geo Insights views. Purchase-related performance is represented through revenue and conversion metrics available in causal Attribution.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Revenue and conversion metrics in Attribution]

## Review purchase-related performance

1. Go to **Measure > Attribution**.
2. Open the **Overview** tab to compare platform-reported and incremental revenue.
3. Open the **Breakdown** tab.
4. Use **Choose columns** to add platform revenue, incremental revenue, platform conversions, incremental conversions, platform CPA, incremental CPA, platform ROAS, and iROAS.
5. Compare results by channel, tactic, campaign, or ad set.

[VIDEO PLACEHOLDER: Adding purchase-related metrics to Breakdown]

## Interpret the results

Platform metrics reflect the values reported by advertising platforms. Incremental metrics estimate the outcomes causally driven by advertising. Reviewing both helps identify where platform reporting may overstate or understate causal impact.

For order-level, product-level, customer-level, or geographic commerce reporting, use the connected commerce or analytics system that owns those records. The causal Attribution workspace is intended for aggregated marketing decisions.
