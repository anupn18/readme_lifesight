# Journeys

The Lifesight 4.0 Attribution workspace focuses on aggregated causal performance and does not include the legacy user-level Journeys tab.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Attribution Overview and Breakdown tabs in Lifesight 4.0]

## Use the 4.0 attribution hierarchy

Use the **Breakdown** tab to analyze performance through the available marketing hierarchy:

- Channel
- Tactic
- Campaign
- Ad Set

Select entities at one level and drill into the next level to understand where spend, incremental revenue, iROAS, conversions, and pacing are concentrated.

[VIDEO PLACEHOLDER: Drilling through the attribution hierarchy]

## Understand the difference

Legacy journey reporting described sequences of user-level touchpoints before conversion. The 4.0 causal workspace answers a different question: how much business impact was incrementally driven by marketing, and how current activity compares with the active plan.

For tactical analysis, compare platform-reported and incremental metrics in Breakdown. For strategic interpretation, use the Overview, promoted Marketing Mix Models, and experiment results together.

> Do not interpret the channel hierarchy as a user-level conversion path. It is an aggregated performance view.
