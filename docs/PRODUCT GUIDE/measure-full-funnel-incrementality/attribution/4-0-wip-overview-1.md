# Overview

The **Overview** tab summarizes causal attribution performance for the selected date range and active target scenario.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Attribution Overview tab]

## Headline metrics

The top cards show:

- **Total Spend**, with elapsed days in the scenario
- **Incremental Revenue**, including its share of platform-reported revenue
- **iROAS**, compared with platform ROAS
- **Net vs Plan**, with overall pacing

## Pacing against plan

The pacing section compares actual spend with planned spend from the active scenario. Review scenario progress, cumulative spend versus plan, and counts of channels that are on track, behind plan, or ahead of plan.

Suggested daily adjustments summarize how much under-pacing channels may need to scale and how much over-pacing channels may need to ease off.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Pacing against plan section]

## Platform-reported versus incremental revenue

This comparison shows what advertising platforms report and what Lifesight estimates was causally incremental. The incrementality factor represents the share of platform-reported revenue estimated to be incremental.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Platform-reported versus incremental revenue card]

## Channel performance

The channel table ranks performance by incremental value. Review spend, incremental revenue, platform ROAS, incrementality factor, pacing, recommendation, and available actions.

[VIDEO PLACEHOLDER: Reviewing channel performance and opening a recommendation]

Use the date picker to update the reporting range. Select the target scenario control to review or change the active benchmark.
