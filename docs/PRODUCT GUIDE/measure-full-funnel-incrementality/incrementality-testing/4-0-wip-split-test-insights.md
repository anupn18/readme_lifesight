# Split Test Insights

Legacy Split Test results are not available in the Lifesight 4.0 experiment workspace. The current results experience supports Geo Experiments.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Geo Experiment Monitoring & Results]

## Available Geo Experiment insights

For a running or completed Geo Experiment, Lifesight can show:

- Experiment progress and spend pacing
- Interim and final lift
- iROAS
- P-value and statistical power
- Confidence interval
- Treatment-versus-control trends
- Test and control market breakdown
- Secondary KPI fit
- Findings and conclusion

[VIDEO PLACEHOLDER: Reviewing completed Geo Experiment insights]

## Use results downstream

Promote credible completed Geo Experiment results when they should support causal Attribution and eligible MMM calibration workflows.

If you need to interpret results from a legacy audience Split Test, retain the original assignment, exposure, KPI, and analysis records and work with your Lifesight representative on the appropriate reporting path.
