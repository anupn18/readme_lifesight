# Geo Experiment Insights

Geo Experiment results in Lifesight 4.0 combine experiment progress, statistical evidence, lift estimates, and market-level interpretation.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Monitoring & Results tab for a Geo Experiment]

## Monitoring and results

During a running experiment, review:

- Experiment window and days elapsed
- Spend pacing
- Interim lift when calculated
- Statistical power
- P-value and significance
- Confidence interval
- Treatment-versus-control trend

Interim results are provisional. Continue monitoring until the planned window and required analysis are complete.

[VIDEO PLACEHOLDER: Reviewing interim analysis and experiment progress]

## Completed results

After final lift calculation, the results can include:

- Measured and adjusted lift
- Measured and adjusted iROAS
- Confidence interval
- P-value and significance
- Test and control market breakdown
- Secondary KPI fit charts
- Findings, recommendation, and conclusion

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Completed lift, iROAS, and confidence cards]

## Design of Experiment

Use the **Design of Experiment** tab to review the original hypothesis, treatment, cells, KPI definitions, date windows, selected markets, power analysis, and synthetic-control fit.

## Post-treatment analysis

When supported, calculate post-treatment lift after the additional observation window. Compare treatment-period and post-treatment results to understand whether effects persisted after the campaign ended.

> Statistical significance is one input to a decision. Review effect size, confidence interval, power, design quality, and business context together.
