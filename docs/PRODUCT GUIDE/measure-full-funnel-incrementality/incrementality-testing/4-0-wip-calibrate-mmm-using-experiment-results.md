# Calibrate MMM Using Experiment Results

Promoted experiment evidence can support Attribution and model calibration in Lifesight 4.0. Use completed Geo Experiment results when the experiment is comparable to the MMM channel, KPI, market, and period you want to calibrate.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Completed Geo Experiment with Promote action]

## Review the experiment result

Before using the result, review:

- Measured lift and confidence interval
- Statistical significance and p-value
- Statistical power
- Estimated iROAS
- Test and control market performance
- Experiment dates, treatment, KPI, and channel
- Any post-treatment result

## Promote the experiment

1. Go to **Measure > Experiments**.
2. Open the completed Geo Experiment.
3. Review **Monitoring & Results** and **Design of Experiment**.
4. Select **Promote** when the result is suitable for downstream use.

[VIDEO PLACEHOLDER: Reviewing and promoting a completed experiment]

Promoting identifies the experiment as approved evidence for supported Attribution and model calibration workflows. Promotion does not change the measured result.

## Apply evidence to MMM

When calibrating or retraining a Marketing Mix Model, review eligible experiment evidence and confirm the channel mapping, date window, iROAS, confidence, and source. Remove evidence that is not comparable to the model.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Experiment evidence shown in an MMM calibration workflow]

> Do not promote an experiment solely because it is complete. Confirm that the design, data quality, statistical result, and business context support its use.
