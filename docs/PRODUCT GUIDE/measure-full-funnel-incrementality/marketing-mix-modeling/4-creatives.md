The Creative module answers three questions: how is each ad performing, is
that performance actually adding revenue, and what should you do next. It has
two tabs. **Intelligence** shows every ad as a card with its performance and a
recommended action. **Incrementality** shows whether each tactic and format
combination is genuinely adding revenue, based on your promoted model.

Intelligence works as soon as your ad data is connected. Incrementality (and
the recommendations that depend on it) unlocks once you promote a model on
the Models page, because incremental returns come from your champion model,
never from a default.

## Intelligence: every ad, scored and actioned

Each card shows the ad's media, its **Creative Strength** score, its key
metrics, and one recommended action.

* **Creative Strength** is a 0 to 100 score of how the ad performs against
  its peers on the same channel and tactic. Tiers: Strong (72+), Solid (50+),
  Watch (30+), Weak (below 30). It measures how the ad performed, not the
  creative in isolation; the info icon on each card explains this.
* **Recommended actions**: Scale (strong and profitable), Refresh (fix or
  rotate the creative), Maintain (leave it running), Pause (stop the ad).
* A low score alone never triggers Pause. An ad is only recommended for
  pausing when it also fails the economics check (it is not returning its
  spend) or its delivery has collapsed from audience fatigue.

## Incrementality: is the result real?

The summary tiles give you the overall picture before you read any rows:

* **Synergy Pairs**: tactic and format combinations where strong creative is
  running on a genuinely incremental tactic.
* **Drag Pairs**: healthy tactics held back by weak creative. Fix the ad,
  keep the tactic.
* **Cannibal Pairs**: combinations where the tactic is re-crediting demand
  you would have gotten anyway.
* **Incremental Revenue**: modelled revenue added by your Synergy pairs.
* **Revenue at Risk**: modelled revenue being forfeited by Drag and Cannibal
  pairs.
* **Saveable Budget**: spend on ads recommended for pausing.
* **Active Spend in Play**: total spend across everything shown.

Numbers marked **MODELED** come from your promoted model. Every tile's
tooltip states exactly how it is calculated, and tiles always add up to the
rows beneath them.

## The matrix

Rows are tactics, columns are asset types (image, video, text). Each cell
shows a state and its action: **Synergy leads to Scale, Neutral to Move, Drag to
Refresh, Cannibal to Stop.** You Stop a tactic and format strategy; you Pause
an individual ad. A Stop cell never pauses everything in it: it resolves to
individual ads, so a strong, profitable ad always stays live. Click any cell
to see its ads in Intelligence, pre-filtered.

## How to use the results

1. Start with the tiles to see the overall balance.
2. Open the Drag and Cannibal cells first; that is where money is leaking.
3. Check the per-ad breakdown before acting. Cells recommend, ads decide.
4. Use Scale recommendations to shift budget toward what is genuinely
   incremental.
5. Treat big findings as hypotheses to test before moving large budgets.

Incrementality figures are modelled relationships from your promoted model.
They complement, not replace, controlled creative testing and platform
delivery analysis.
