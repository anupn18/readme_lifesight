The **Insights** tab turns model output into focused analyses of media performance, baseline demand, trend, and seasonality. Use it after reviewing the model's Data, Diagnostics, Graph, and Contribution tabs.

**[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Insights tab showing the analysis view selector and key insight cards]**

## Select the Analysis View

Use the view control to move between the available analysis groups. The exact analyses depend on the variables and components present in the selected model.

### Media Analyses

Media analyses highlight changes in paid activity and incremental performance. Use them to identify channels or tactics with material shifts in spend, contribution, or efficiency.

### Baseline Analyses

Baseline analyses explain the non-media portion of the outcome. They can include organic, contextual, and other baseline factors included in the model.

### Trend Analyses

Trend analyses identify sustained movement in the outcome that is not explained by short-term media activity. Review these results alongside business changes, distribution, pricing, and macro conditions.

### Seasonality Analyses

Seasonality analyses show recurring calendar patterns. Use the calendar and decomposition views to distinguish seasonal movement from media-driven contribution.

## Key Insights

Each analysis presents a concise observation supported by the selected model and date range. Depending on the insight, you may see:

* Direction and magnitude of change
* The affected channel, tactic, or baseline factor
* Comparison with a prior period
* A supporting time series, decomposition, matrix, or ranked view
* A recommended area for investigation

> 📘 Insights summarize model output. Validate important findings in the Contribution, Graph, and Diagnostics tabs before changing strategy.

## Contribution Analysis

For detailed channel and tactic metrics, open the **Contribution** tab. It includes:

* Incremental outcome and efficiency
* Spend and contribution share
* Confidence intervals
* Causal evidence
* Immediate and carryover effects
* Profit metrics where cost settings are available
* Saturation curves and marginal efficiency

Use the page date range for the contribution table. Use the independent Period selector for saturation curves and marginal efficiency fields.

## Baseline Profile

Baseline profile views help explain how non-media variables contribute to the modelled outcome. Review the summary, factor table, and matrix to identify the strongest baseline drivers and how their effects vary across the selected period.

## Comparing Periods

Where comparison controls are available, compare the selected range with a previous period. Look for changes that are large enough to be meaningful and consistent with the model's confidence and diagnostic health.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Select the model and analysis date range.
2. Review the highest-priority insight cards.
3. Open the supporting analysis for context.
4. Validate channel results in Contribution.
5. Check causal paths in Graph.
6. Confirm model health in Diagnostics.
7. Use Planner to test a budget change before promoting a decision.
