The **Insights** tab organizes model output into **Media Analyses** and **Baseline Analyses**. It is hidden by default and can be enabled from **Customize Tabs**.

**[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Insights tab showing Media Analyses and Baseline Analyses]**

## Media Analyses

Switch between channel and tactic views where available.

### Contribution waterfall

The waterfall shows how media variables build up to the selected outcome. Use it to identify the largest contributors and understand the relative scale of each component.

### Efficiency grid

The grid compares contribution and efficiency. Channels with high contribution and strong efficiency may be important current drivers. Channels with low contribution but strong efficiency may warrant a closer look at scale, uncertainty, and saturation.

### Ranking chart

The ranking view shows how channel or tactic positions change across the selected periods. Focus on sustained movement, then check whether spend or efficiency explains the change.

### Contribution heatmap

The heatmap shows how contribution changes over time. Use it to identify periods of concentration, recurring patterns, and changes that should be compared with campaign activity.

## Baseline Analyses

Baseline analyses explain the non-media portion of the modelled outcome.

* **Baseline factors** lists the variables that contribute to baseline demand.
* **Relative baseline** compares the scale of the available baseline components.
* **Trend lift** shows longer-term movement associated with the modelled trend.
* **Decomposition** shows how baseline components change over time.
* **Seasonality and holiday analysis** highlights recurring calendar patterns and configured events.

## Validate an insight

Insights are analysis views, not standalone recommendations. Validate a media finding in Contribution, review its causal paths in Graph, and check model health in Diagnostics. For baseline findings, compare the timing with known pricing, distribution, promotional, seasonal, and market events.
