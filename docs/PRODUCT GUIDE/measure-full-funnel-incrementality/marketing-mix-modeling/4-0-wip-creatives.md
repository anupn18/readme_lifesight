The **Creatives** tab shows how creative combinations interact within the selected model. It is hidden by default and can be enabled from **Customize Tabs** when creative output is available.

**[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Creatives tab with summary metrics and interaction matrix]**

## Summary

The summary can include positive interaction, cannibalization, incremental revenue, and revenue loss. Use it to understand the overall balance of creative combinations before reviewing individual rows.

Positive interaction indicates combinations associated with a stronger joint result. Cannibalization indicates combinations where the joint result is weaker than expected.

## Matrix and table views

Use the matrix for a visual overview of creative interactions. Switch to the table to review detailed fields such as channel, quality score, base efficiency, multiplier, and top creative.

* **Quality score** summarizes the strength or reliability of the available creative signal.
* **Base efficiency** is the modelled efficiency before the interaction adjustment.
* **Multiplier** shows how the interaction changes the base result.
* **Top creative** identifies the strongest creative in the available grouping.

## How to use the results

1. Start with the summary to understand the overall effect.
2. Use the matrix to locate strong positive or negative combinations.
3. Open the table and review the channel, quality, efficiency, and multiplier together.
4. Check campaign timing, audience overlap, format, and creative strategy for context.
5. Turn important findings into testable hypotheses before changing investment.

Creative interactions are modelled relationships. They do not replace controlled creative testing or platform delivery analysis.
