# Interpreting a Plan

Planner results show how a scenario changes allocation, forecasted outcomes, and incremental efficiency relative to the selected baseline.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Completed Planner scenario results]

## Headline metrics

Review total budget, forecasted outcome, incremental outcome, and forecasted efficiency. Efficiency appears as iROAS for revenue-oriented models or iCPA for conversion-oriented models.

If configuration has changed since the last run, results remain stale until you run the simulation again.

## Recommendations

The recommendations table compares current and recommended values by channel. Review:

- Current and recommended budget
- Budget delta
- Current and forecasted incremental outcome
- Current and optimized efficiency
- Induced or mediator effects when available

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Channel recommendations table]

## Forecast

The forecast chart compares actual or baseline performance with the optimized forecast and its confidence bounds. The monthly table shows baseline, forecasted outcome, confidence interval, and cumulative outcome.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Forecast chart and monthly forecast table]

## Budget worksheet and pacing

Use the Budget Worksheet to review allocation across platforms and periods. The Pacing Breakdown shows how budget is distributed over the plan window, including custom pacing when configured.

## Saturation curves

Saturation curves show the relationship between spend and response for supported channels. Compare current and optimized spend markers, constraint ranges, marginal ROAS, and break-even points.

[VIDEO PLACEHOLDER: Reviewing recommendations, forecast, and saturation curves]

## Compare scenarios

Switch between scenario tabs and compare one material change at a time. Confirm that the model, plan period, and reference period remain comparable before attributing differences to a budget or constraint change.

Save the plan after all scenarios have completed, then promote the scenario selected for Decisions.
