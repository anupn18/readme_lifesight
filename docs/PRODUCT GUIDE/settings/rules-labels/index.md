Attribution involves assigning credit for conversions (such as sales or leads) to different marketing channels, campaigns, or touchpoints that a customer interacts with before making a purchase. This process often relies on tracking UTM parameters.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/941d03b6c4f7513a77b8bc8c3b3b90616e1ecef97b41532848c568f11c5a60cd-ru.jpg" />

The absence of a streamlined approach for handling UTM source, medium, and campaign values results in a disorganized and cluttered data display within the Attribution interface. Rules and Labeling seeks to address this issue by offering a user-friendly solution in the settings section for straightforward labeling, organizing, and verification of these UTM values.

**The feature enables users to:**

* **Data Organization and Insight Generation:** Tailor data organization, grouping, and labeling to fit an organization's unique naming standards, facilitating the discovery of deeper marketing insights.
* **Customization and Oversight:** Provides the capability to bypass the default data arrangement logic of Lifesight, empowering users to structure data in line with their specific organizational requirements.
* **Variable Comparison:** Facilitates the comparison of diverse variables to conduct a detailed analysis of the factors most significantly influencing advertising campaign outcomes.
