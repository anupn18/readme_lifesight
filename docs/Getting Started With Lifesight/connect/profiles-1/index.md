Customer Profiles are comprehensive individual records that centralize and organize all the relevant information about a customer in one place. These profiles are essential for providing a holistic view of each customer, enabling businesses to deliver personalized experiences, improve engagement, and optimize marketing efforts. By aggregating data from multiple sources, such as customer interactions, purchase history, and behavior patterns, profiles help businesses understand their audience at a deeper level.
