Lifesight is built for consumer brands that want causal, finance‑ready marketing decisions—from board‑level budget allocation to day‑to‑day optimization—without depending on user‑level tracking. If you care about incremental ROI, profit‑aligned forecasting, and operational guardrails grounded in real lift, you’re in the right place.

<br />

The teams we serve
By role

CMO & VP of Growth – Set mix and targets, defend budgets, balance brand vs. performance with causal proof.

CFO & FP&A – Tie marketing to revenue/margin, evaluate risk/return with confidence ranges and payback windows.

Performance & Media – Optimize campaigns using incrementality‑adjusted KPIs, not raw platform numbers.

Analytics & Insights – Govern data contracts, MMM quality, and a disciplined geo‑test roadmap.

By industry

DTC & E‑commerce – SKU/offer cadence, promo/price effects, and scaling prospecting beyond retargeting.

Retail & CPG – Omnichannel readouts (e‑comm + retail/POS), CTV/YouTube and retailer media effects.

Consumer Services & Apps – LTV/payback targets, creative‑audience testing, and privacy‑safe measurement.

What Lifesight helps you achieve

Measure full‑funnel incrementality across channels, tactics, geos, and time.

Forecast to achieve profit and growth with uncertainty bands and clear trade‑offs.

Optimize channel and campaign spend using marginal returns and lift‑calibrated KPIs.

Analyze with one causal truth that aligns marketing and finance.

The “fit” checklist

You’re a strong fit if most of these are true:

Decisions at stake: You’re making quarterly budget or monthly re‑plan calls, not just in‑channel tweaks.

Learning culture: You’re willing to run or accept geo tests and to calibrate reporting with lift.

Data access: You can provide spend and outcome data at least weekly, plus basic context (seasonality, promos).

Multi‑channel mix: You invest across 2+ paid channels (e.g., Meta, Search, YouTube/CTV, Retail Media, Influencer).

Finance alignment: You track payback, profit, or contribution—not only platform ROAS.

Privacy posture: You prefer aggregate/geo‑safe methods over user‑level tracking dependencies.

Edge cases that still work well with Lifesight

Early‑stage brands with limited history but willingness to test—start with geo tests and incrementality‑calibrated attribution; grow into MMM as data accrues.

Large offline revenue components—bring retail/POS and regional variation; we read halo and omnichannel effects.

Not yet a fit if

You need a real‑time, user‑level single source of truth for all attribution (we calibrate with causal truth; we don’t do identity‑level stitch).

You won’t run or accept experiments and only want to optimize to last‑click or platform‑reported ROAS.

You cannot access any reliable outcome data (orders/revenue/subscriptions) or basic marketing spend.

Minimum data to get started

Good (minimum viable)

Outcomes: Orders/revenue (weekly or daily), new vs. returning if available.

Media: Channel‑level spend (weekly) for your active paid channels.

Context: Seasonality/calendar (e.g., holidays, sales events).

Attribution hygiene: UTMs/rules and consistent naming to map platforms cleanly.

Better

Promo/price calendars, product/inventory flags, geo splits (where you sell/ship), retail/POS rollups, and high‑level creative or audience tags.

Best

Additional demand drivers (e.g., competitor or macro proxies), brand/traffic indicators (share of search, sessions), and richer geo/campaign granularity.

Common questions

Do we need a huge budget to benefit?
No. Variation and willingness to test matter more than sheer spend. Smaller teams can start with geo tests + calibrated attribution and progress to MMM.

Will this replace GA4 or platform dashboards?
No. We reconcile and calibrate them so your daily decisions reflect incrementality, then we roll that truth up for planning and finance.

Is user‑level tracking required?
No. Lifesight is privacy‑safe by design—we rely on aggregate and geo‑level methods, plus platform data that you already have.

How much history do we need for MMM?
More is better, but you can begin with recent, clean months across at least two active channels and expand as data grows. Geo tests help accelerate confidence.
