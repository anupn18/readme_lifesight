# How Lifesight Works

Lifesight turns fragmented marketing data into a single, causal decision system.

At a high level, the platform unifies three complementary methods—Marketing Mix Modeling, Geo‑based Incrementality Testing, and Incrementality‑Adjusted Attribution—and channels them into an Action layer for planning and optimization. The result is one loop where every insight can be proven, every plan simulated, and every daily decision aligned to business outcomes.

## The operating loop

Lifesight runs a continuous loop that connects strategy, testing, and execution:

*   **Model** – Quantify what’s driving results across all channels and contexts.
*   **Test** – Prove causal lift where it matters most.
*   **Calibrate** – Correct reporting and models using experimental truth.
*   **Plan** – Create forecasted scenarios and recommendations you can act on.
*   **Optimize** – Make day‑to‑day decisions with incrementality-adjusted KPIs.
*   **Monitor** – Track realized vs. forecasted results and refresh the loop.

This loop ensures you’re never choosing between speed and truth—you get both.

## Platform architecture (high level)

1.  **Data Foundation**
    *   **Sources**: Media platforms, web/app analytics, ecommerce/POS, CRM/subscription, price & promo calendars, product/inventory, and context (seasonality, geography, macro).
    *   **Standardization**: Unified taxonomies, data contracts, and QA checks ensure comparability across channels and time.
    *   **Privacy‑first**: Aggregated and pseudonymized where appropriate; no dependency on third‑party cookies.

2.  **Measurement Layer**
    *   **Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM)**: Long‑ and short‑term effectiveness, channel contribution, and marginal returns.
    *   **Geo‑based Incrementality Testing**: Controlled, scalable tests to quantify causal lift for channels, tactics, audiences, and offers.
    *   **Incrementality‑Adjusted Attribution**: Granular, always‑on reporting calibrated with lift factors so daily reads reflect causal reality.

3.  **Action Layer**
    *   **Scenario Planner**: Forecast the outcomes of different budget mixes and pacing strategies under uncertainty.
    *   **Recommendations & Guardrails**: Translate curves and lift evidence into channel/tactic guidance that respects constraints (e.g., floors/ceilings, lead times).
    *   **Exports & Integrations**: Move plans into your buying stack and workflows with auditability.

4.  **Analytics & Governance**
    *   **Dashboards**: Executive to analyst‑level views linking spend, contribution, and progress to targets.
    *   **Versioning & Audit Trails**: Every model, test, and plan is traceable to inputs and assumptions.
    *   **Refresh & Drift Monitoring**: Cadence for re‑training models and re‑testing to keep guidance accurate.

## What each method contributes

*   **MMM → Strategic clarity**
    Sets the stage for quarterly planning, shows diminishing returns, and identifies the biggest levers to move.
*   **Geo Tests → Causal proof**
    Validate what’s working, de‑risk new bets, and quantify true lift to correct bias in models and platform reports.
*   **Adjusted Attribution → Daily control**
    Keeps channel and campaign teams aligned with causal truth while they optimize creatives, audiences, and bids.

These methods reinforce one another: tests sharpen the models; models identify where to test; attribution inherits the truth from both.

## Inputs in, decisions out

### Inputs

*   **Minimum viable**: spend by channel, outcome KPIs (orders/revenue/subscriptions), core site/app metrics, and seasonality.
*   **Better with**: price & promo calendars, retail/POS roll‑ups, product/inventory, creative/placement metadata, geography, and macro signals.

### Decisions you can make

*   **Budget allocation**: Where to move the next dollar for the highest incremental return.
*   **Channel & tactic scaling**: Which plays to scale, cap, or pause based on proven lift and marginal effects.
*   **New channel launches**: Pilot design, expected outcomes, and success criteria before you scale.
*   **Creative & audience priorities**: Day‑to‑day optimization using calibrated KPIs instead of raw platform metrics.

## Cadence you can run

*   **Quarterly**: Refresh MMM, re‑forecast, and set profit‑aligned budgets; kick off 1–2 flagship geo tests.
*   **Monthly**: Compare realized vs. forecast, run scenario updates, adjust constraints, and review test results.
*   **Weekly/Daily**: Optimize campaigns using incrementality‑adjusted attribution; monitor pacing against plan.
*   **Annually**: Revisit your learning agenda, long‑term effects, and portfolio strategy.

## What “good” looks like

*   **One source of decision truth**: The same numbers guide the CMO, CFO, and channel leads—at the right level of detail for each.
*   **Proven lift informs all moves**: Tests confirm winners; their lift calibrates both models and daily KPIs.
*   **Plans with uncertainty bands**: Recommendations come with ranges and trade‑offs, not false precision.
*   **Tight feedback loops**: Realized vs. forecasted results are reviewed routinely, and the loop refreshes.

## Privacy, security & reliability (at a glance)

*   **Privacy‑safe by design**: Works on aggregated and pseudonymized data; no reliance on cross‑site identifiers.
*   **Transparent methods**: Clear assumptions, versioned models, and documented test designs.
*   **Operational resilience**: Data quality checks, anomaly detection, and rollback paths for plans and models.

## Getting started

*   Connect your core data (commerce outcomes + paid media) and confirm taxonomy.
*   Run a baseline MMM to see contribution and marginal effects.
*   Launch a priority geo test where stakes are high or confidence is low.
*   Calibrate attribution with lift factors to align daily optimization.
*   Use the planner to reallocate budget and track realized vs. forecast.
