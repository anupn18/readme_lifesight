The Lifesight Partner Program is a comprehensive solution for agencies aiming to provide data-driven marketing insights to their clients. With access to the full suite of Lifesight’s marketing measurement tools, partners can offer their clients a competitive edge, enhance ROI, and streamline reporting. The program is backed by dedicated support, exclusive resources, and brand count based pricing model.

***

## Benefits of Joining the Lifesight Partner Program

### Access to Lifesight’s Platform

- Gain full access to Lifesight’s advanced marketing measurement and optimization platform, including features such as:
  - Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM)
  - Incrementality Testing (Experiments)
  - Causal Attribution

### Exclusive Partner Resources

- Partner training programs and certifications.
- Exclusive access to case studies, research, and insights.
- Early access to new feature releases and product updates.

### Dedicated Partner Support

- Priority technical and customer support.
- Access to a dedicated account manager to ensure successful onboarding and usage.

***

## Key Features of Lifesight for Agencies

### Unified Marketing Measurement

- Measure the full customer journey and attribute conversions to marketing touchpoints.
- Customize attribution models to suit specific client needs.

### Marketing Mix Modeling

- Predict the impact of various marketing activities on overall business performance.
- Optimize marketing budget allocation for clients across channels like TV, digital, radio, and more.

### Incrementality Testing

- Determine the true incremental impact of campaigns.
- Run tests to measure the effectiveness of new channels or marketing strategies.

### Data Integration

- Easily integrate with popular marketing platforms such as Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Klaviyo, and more.
- Sync Lifesight data with your client’s CRM for holistic analysis.

***

## Partner Support

### Technical Support

- 24/7 priority access to Lifesight’s support team for troubleshooting via email or chat (English only)
- Get technical assistance with platform setup, integrations, and performance optimization while onboarding.
- Get dedicated marketing science services while onboarding to make the most of Lifesight and drive results for your clients.

### Partner Success Team

- Dedicated Partner Success Managers to assist in driving value from Lifesight for both your agency and clients.
- Regular updates on new features, best practices, and success strategies.
