Touch-based attribution gives credit for a conversion to the marketing touchpoints a customer interacted with before converting. A touchpoint can be an ad click, an ad view, an email, or a website visit from any channel.

Each method answers the same question in a different way: which touchpoints should get credit, and how much? The right choice depends on your sales cycle, the channels you run, and the decisions you need to make.

## Attribution methods

| Method                                                                              | How credit is assigned                                                      | Best for                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Single-Touch Attribution](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/single-touch-attribution) | 100% to the first or last touchpoint                                        | Quick, simple reads on what starts or closes journeys                 |
| [Multi-Touch Attribution](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/multi-touch-attribution)   | Shared across all touchpoints, using Time Decay, U-Shaped, or Linear models | Understanding how channels work together across the journey           |
| [View Through Attribution](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/view-through-attribution) | To ad impressions that led to direct or organic visits without a click      | Measuring the impact of video, display, and other view-heavy channels |
| [Algorithmic Attribution](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/algorithmic-attribution)   | Based on patterns in your data rather than fixed rules                      | Data-driven credit allocation across complex journeys                 |

## Choosing a method

* **Short, simple journeys:** Single-Touch Attribution is easy to set up and read.
* **Several touchpoints before conversion:** Multi-Touch Attribution shows how each channel contributes.
* **Large spend on impressions:** View Through Attribution captures impact that click-based methods miss.
* **Complex journeys across many channels:** Algorithmic Attribution lets your data decide how credit is shared.

You can use more than one method side by side to compare how channel credit shifts.

## Things to keep in mind

Touch-based attribution shows which touchpoints were present before a conversion. It does not on its own prove that a touchpoint caused the conversion. To measure true incremental impact, pair these methods with [Causal Attribution](#) and [Incrementality Testing](#).
