Deploy records every change applied to your campaigns. Whether a change came from a recommendation or from a value you typed yourself, the Logs tab keeps a transparent, auditable record.

You always know:

* What changed, with before and after values.
* Who made the change, a workspace member or the system.
* When it happened.
* Why it happened, linked to the promoted plan and the recommendation it came from.

## The Logs tab

All applied changes are recorded in Logs, reachable from the Deploy header. Each entry includes:

* Entity: the campaign or ad set updated.
* Field: what changed, for example budget, bid target or status.
* Previous value and new value.
* Status: applied successfully, or flagged with a warning.
* User: the workspace member who applied it, or the system.
* Timestamp: the exact date and time.

## Filters and search

Logs can be filtered to find the changes you care about:

* By user, to see changes applied by you or a teammate.
* By channel, to focus on a single platform.
* By plan, to review actions tied to a specific plan.
* By time, to narrow to a date range.

Logs can be exported for reporting or audit.

## Why logging matters

Transparency, so you know exactly how Deploy is shaping your campaigns. Collaboration, so teams can see who applied what. Accountability, so there is an audit trail for finance or leadership review. Confidence, so no adjustment is a black box.

Only Managers and Admins can apply changes. When they do, every action is logged and visible to the whole workspace.
