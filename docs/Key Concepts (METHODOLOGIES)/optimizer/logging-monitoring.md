The Optimizer provides full visibility into every change applied to your campaigns. Whether recommendations were applied automatically or manually, the **Logs tab** gives you a transparent, auditable record.

This ensures you always know:

* **What changed** (before and after values)
* **Who made the change** (system or workspace member)
* **When it happened**
* **Why it happened** (linked to the Default Scenario and Optimizer recommendations)

### The Logs Tab

All applied recommendations are recorded in the **Logs tab**, accessible from the Optimizer module.

Each log entry includes:

* **Entity** – The campaign or ad set updated
* **Field** – What changed (e.g., budget, bid target, status)
* **Previous Value → New Value** – Before and after comparison
* **Status** – Applied successfully or flagged with a warning
* **User** – Whether it was applied by a Manager/Admin or the system
* **Timestamp** – Exact date and time of the change

### Filters and Search

Logs can be filtered to help you quickly find the updates you care about:

* **By User** – See only the changes applied by you or your teammates
* **By Channel** – Focus on a single platform (e.g., Google, Meta)
* **By Scenario** – Review actions tied to a specific plan scenario
* **By Time** – Narrow down to a particular date range

> 📘 _Tip:_ You can export logs for reporting or auditing purposes.

### Why Logging Matters

* **Transparency** → You know exactly how Optimizer is shaping your campaigns.
* **Collaboration** → Teams can see who applied which changes, reducing confusion.
* **Accountability** → Provides an audit trail for finance or leadership reviews.
* **Confidence** → Ensures recommendations aren’t a black box — every adjustment is recorded.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Only **Managers and Admins** can apply changes. When they do, every action is logged, so the entire workspace has visibility.
