A **live artifact** is a dashboard that reads your workspace data every time someone opens it. Share it once, and everyone who opens it later sees the latest figures, without anyone rebuilding a report. Live dashboards carry the **Live** tag in your library.

![A live dashboard built from the CMO template: the toolbar, a banner for changes not yet saved, the brand and title, the summary, and the date and channel controls](https://files.readme.io/b404cba2f678c5fc41d9c2f6a962a088bbec146b89acc61737d2e8470f382230-13-live-dashboard.png)

***

## Make a live dashboard

There are two ways:

| Way               | Use it when                                                     | How                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| From a template   | You want a proven layout: the CMO, CFO or Creatives dashboard.  | In the template gallery, select **Use template** on a live template. See [Templates](doc:4-0-wip-artifact-templates).           |
| By describing it  | You want your own mix of figures, charts and tables.            | On the **Artifacts** page, ask for it, for example "Create a live dashboard of spend, incremental revenue and iROAS by channel". |

Either way, Lifesight reads your data for each tile and opens the dashboard as a draft.

***

## Save a draft to your library

A new dashboard starts as a **draft**. The banner across the top says **Only you can see this dashboard. Save it to add it to your library.**

![The draft banner under a dashboard's toolbar, with Save to library](https://files.readme.io/152d67b8f9f1f38efa19a287bb6e6318583b15533b455bf7b45bc9b044f84757-14-live-draft-banner.png)

1. Check the dashboard.
2. Select **Save to library**.
3. In **Name your dashboard**, keep the name Lifesight suggests or type your own.
4. Select **Save**.

The dashboard joins your library, and **Share** and **Pin** appear in its toolbar.

<Image align="center" width="380px" alt="The Name your dashboard dialog, with the suggested name selected" src="https://files.readme.io/4c2ca9cefd7594d18909a5191b342ee9e251c4fa35c53ffbc57012f8c07fdf60-15-live-save.png" />

<Callout icon="📘" theme="info">
  **Note:** A draft you don't save expires after 30 days. To get it back after that, ask Lifesight to build it again.
</Callout>

## Read a live dashboard

From top to bottom, a live dashboard has:

- **Your brand and the title.** Your workspace's logo and brand name sit beside the dashboard's title. See [Brand Kit](doc:4-0-wip-brand-kit).
- **A summary.** One or two sentences with the headline figures. Select **Ask why** to have Lifesight explain them.
- **Controls.** The choices this dashboard offers, such as **From** and **To** dates, a model, or the channels to include. The line on the right says which dates the figures cover and when the data runs to.
- **Tiles.** Figures, charts and tables, grouped into sections. Select **⋯** on a tile and then **Ask Lifesight why** to have its figures explained.

A dashboard built from the CMO template also shows **What needs you**, the cards from your Cockpit that are waiting on you, and ends with **Data behind this page**, the sources it reads. The sections of each template are listed in [Templates](doc:4-0-wip-artifact-templates#choose-a-template).

### Choose the dates

1. Open **From** and pick a date.
2. Open **To** and pick a date.
3. Select **Apply**.

The tiles read your data again for the new dates. The dates are kept in the page's address, so a link you copy opens with the same dates. A tile whose figures don't follow a control says so, for example **Not filtered by From and To**.

### Use the toolbar

| Control                        | What it does                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Updated …**                  | When the figures were last read.                                                                                     |
| **Refresh now**                | Reads your data again. Your layout stays as it is.                                                                   |
| **Data behind each tile**      | Shows, under each tile, where its figures come from and when they were read. Select **Sources** to see every read and every calculation on the page. Press **Esc** to hide it again. |
| **Recheck access and data**    | Checks again what you can see and which sources are available, then redraws the dashboard.                           |
| **Share**                      | Invites people or shares with the workspace. See [Share a dashboard](#share-a-dashboard).                            |
| **Pin**                        | **Pin for me** or **Pin for workspace**, to keep the dashboard in the sidebar.                                       |
| **⋯** (More actions)           | **Delete dashboard**.                                                                                                |

## Change a dashboard by asking

You change a live dashboard by asking for what you want, not by dragging tiles.

1. On the open dashboard, select **Ask Lifesight** at the top right.
2. Ask for the change. For example: "Remove the Notes tile", "Show only the top 10 campaigns by overspend", or "Add iROAS by channel".
3. Lifesight updates the open dashboard in place.

On a saved dashboard, your changes start as **Unsaved changes** that only you can see. Select **Save changes** to show them to everyone who has access.

You can also ask questions about what the dashboard shows. A question gets an answer in the panel and leaves the dashboard as it is.

<Callout icon="📘" theme="info">
  **Tip:** Lifesight builds tiles only from data your workspace has. Ask for figures from your connected sources, models, plans and experiments.
</Callout>

***

## Share a dashboard

Select **Share** in the dashboard's toolbar.

- **Invite people:** type an email address, choose **Can view** or **Can edit**, and select **Send invite**. **Quick add** suggests teammates.
- **Share with the whole workspace:** select **+** beside **Share with workspace**.

A live dashboard is shared with people in your workspace, so everyone who opens it sees the figures their own access allows. Live dashboards have no public link; to send figures outside your workspace, ask Lifesight for a PDF report or a deck. See [Artifacts](doc:4-0-wip-artifacts#share-an-artifact-with-the-right-people).

![The Share this artifact dialog for a live dashboard, with invitations and Share with workspace](https://files.readme.io/eb1af4deabec1eb01346d5af3fe9779aa1f63615b0b515f1300c0b1717ab68cf-16-live-share.png)

People who can view but not change a dashboard see **View only** in its toolbar. They can still ask questions about what it shows, and its owner can give them edit access from **Share**.

***

## Delete a dashboard

1. Select **⋯** in the toolbar, then **Delete dashboard**.
2. Confirm.

Deleting removes the dashboard for everyone who can see it, at once, and can't be undone. Only its owner can delete it. Deleting a draft removes only your draft.

## Frequently asked questions about Live Artifacts

### What is a live artifact?

A live artifact is a dashboard that reads your workspace data every time it opens, so it always shows the latest figures. It carries the **Live** tag in your library.

### How is a live dashboard different from a report or deck?

A live dashboard reads your data each time it opens. A web page, PDF report or deck that Lifesight made keeps the figures it was made with. Use a dashboard for something you check often, and a report or deck for a snapshot to send or present.

### Why can't my team see my new dashboard?

It's probably still a draft. Drafts are visible only to you. Select **Save to library**, then share it.

### How long does a draft last?

30 days. Save it to your library to keep it.

### How do I refresh the figures?

They're read every time the dashboard opens. To read them again while it's open, select **Refresh now**.

### Can I drag tiles around or resize them?

No. Ask for the change in **Ask Lifesight** instead, for example "Move the channel table to the top".

### What does Data behind each tile show?

Under each tile, where its figures come from and when they were read. **Sources** lists every read and every calculation on the page, so you can check any figure.

### Why does a tile say Not filtered by From and To?

That tile's figures don't change with those controls. The other tiles on the page do.

### Why do some dashboards show a step to take instead of figures?

Dashboards built from templates draw more as your workspace grows. A tile that needs a step first, such as a promoted model or a saved plan, says which step it's waiting on. See [Templates](doc:4-0-wip-artifact-templates#what-each-template-needs).

### Can I share a live dashboard outside my workspace?

No. Live dashboards are shared with people in your workspace. To send figures outside, ask Lifesight for a PDF report or a deck and create a public link for it.

### Who can delete a dashboard?

Only its owner.

### Can I use my brand's colors and logo?

Yes. Every live dashboard in the workspace uses the brand kit a Workspace Admin sets up in **Settings > Brand Kit**. See [Brand Kit](doc:4-0-wip-brand-kit).

### Why does my workspace say dashboards aren't turned on?

Live dashboards are turned on per workspace. A Workspace Admin can ask Lifesight to turn them on.
