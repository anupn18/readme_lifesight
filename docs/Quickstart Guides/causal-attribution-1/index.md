Causal Attribution shows you which channels, campaigns, and ad sets are actually growing your business, so you can shift budget toward what works and pull back from what doesn't. In Lifesight, it brings incremental measurement and your active media plan into one place, so you always know both what is driving outcomes and whether your spend is on track.

Unlike touch-based attribution, which hands out credit based on clicks and views, Causal Attribution is built on evidence. Incremental results come from your promoted Marketing Mix Models and experiment results, giving you a view of performance you can confidently act on.

## Make Confident, Evidence-Backed Budget Decisions

**With Causal Attribution, you can:**

- **Separate real impact from reported credit&#x20;**&#x62;y comparing platform-reported revenue with incremental revenue
- **Understand true efficiency at a glance&#x20;**&#x77;ith total spend, iROAS, incrementality factor (the share of platform-reported revenue that is truly incremental), and net spend versus plan
- **Catch overspend and underspend early** by monitoring pacing against your active scenario
- **Find exactly where performance is won or lost&#x20;**&#x62;y analyzing results from channel down to tactic (the strategy or ad type within a channel, such as prospecting or retargeting), campaign, and ad set.
- **Know where to invest** next with clear Scale, Maintain, and Reduce recommendations.
- **Turn insight into action&#x20;**&#x62;y deploying a recommendation straight into the Decisions workflow

## Get a Clear Picture of Performance and Pacing in One View

The **Overview&#x20;**&#x67;ives you a snapshot of how your marketing is performing for the selected date range, so you can quickly spot what needs attention.

Here you'll find your headline metrics, how your spend is pacing against plan, and a side-by-side comparison of platform-reported and incremental revenue.&#x20;

Channels are ranked by incremental value, so the ones contributing the most real growth rise to the top and the ones that look better on paper than in practice are easy to identify.

## Pinpoint What's Working at Every Level

The **Breakdown&#x20;**&#x6C;ets you go beyond channel totals to find the specific tactics, campaigns, and ad sets driving incremental results.

You can:

- Search for any channel, tactic, campaign, or ad set.
- Choose the columns that matter most to your analysis.
- Sort by any metric to surface your top and bottom performers.
- Select rows to drill deeper into the entities behind a result.
- Open weekly spend details to see how investment has changed over time.

This makes it easier to explain why a channel is performing the way it is and to decide exactly where to adjust.

## Keep Spend Aligned With Your Plan

Your active target scenario (the media plan you've selected as your benchmark) sets the goals that pacing and recommendations are measured against. It determines both the benchmark value and the planned spend, so every insight is tied back to what you set out to achieve.

To see what's shaping your results, select the scenario control. You can review the metrics being tracked, the benchmark value, and the analysis periods in use.

> Get the most accurate recommendations: The more measurement and scenario data your workspace has, the stronger and more reliable your causal recommendations will be. Keeping your Marketing Mix Models promoted, running experiments, and maintaining an active scenario ensures your recommendations reflect how your marketing truly performs.
