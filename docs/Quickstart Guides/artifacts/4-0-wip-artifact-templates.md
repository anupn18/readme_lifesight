Templates are ready-made dashboards, reports and decks. Pick one, and Lifesight fills it with your own workspace data, so you get a proven layout without describing it from scratch.

To open the gallery, go to **Artifacts** and select **See all** next to **Templates**. Type in **Search templates** to find one by name.

## Choose a template

The tag on each card tells you what you'll get: a **Live Page** (a live dashboard), a **Static Page** (a web page), a **PDF** or a **PPT** (a PowerPoint deck).

| Template                          | You get            | What it covers                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CMO Dashboard**                 | Live dashboard     | Revenue, spend and return across every channel, the plan in force and how it's pacing, and what's waiting on you. Sections: **What needs you**, **Channels**, **The plan**, **What the tests confirmed**, **Data behind this page**. |
| **CFO Dashboard**                 | Live dashboard     | Marketing's P&L, spend against budget and its pace to month end, the plan's forecast and gain, and the decisions and risks waiting. Sections: **Marketing P&L**, **Budget against the plan**, **Decisions and risks**, **Can the numbers be trusted**. |
| **Creatives**                     | Live dashboard     | Creative spend and return by tactic and asset, and how much of your creative data covers engagement and fatigue.                                                                                                   |
| **Pacing Recommendations**        | Web page           | A pacing summary, actual spend and pacing by channel, the constraints and evidence behind them, and recommendations.                                                                                               |
| **QBR Deck**                      | PowerPoint deck    | A quarterly business review in eleven sections, from the executive summary, incremental outcome, spend and return, and channel performance to the plan outlook and decisions, with sources and presenter notes on the slides. |
| **Ad Channel Performance Report** | PDF report         | A performance summary, spend and performance by channel, trends, incremental performance, what the measurement can't tell you, and recommended next steps.                                                         |
| **Audiences**                     | Web page           | An audience summary, conversions by segment, who converts, what the data covers, and next steps.                                                                                                                  |
| **Brand Health**                  | PDF report         | Brand demand, its trend, reach and efficiency, what the evidence can't establish yet, and the next evidence and decisions.                                                                                         |

## Use a template

Select **Use template** on the card you want. What happens next depends on what the template makes.

### A live dashboard template

1. Select **Use template**.
2. The **Building your CMO Dashboard** page opens while Lifesight reads your workspace data for each tile. This can take a minute.
3. The dashboard opens as a draft that only you can see.
4. Select **Save to library** and name it to keep it.

Each **Use template** makes a new draft, so you can build the same template again later without changing the dashboards you've already saved. See [Live Artifacts](doc:4-0-wip-live-artifacts#save-a-draft-to-your-library).

### A web page, PDF or deck template

1. Select **Use template**.
2. A conversation opens in **Ask Lifesight** with the template's request already sent.
3. Lifesight reads your workspace data and builds the file. When it's done, the reply shows it with **Open in Artifacts**.

The file is already in your library, tagged **Static**. To change it, keep asking in the same conversation; each change is saved as a new version.

## What each template needs

Templates use what your workspace already has, and the live dashboards draw more as your workspace grows.

| Template          | What it needs to draw in full                                                                                                       |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CMO Dashboard** | Connected ad platforms for spend, a promoted model for incremental revenue and return, a promoted plan for pacing, and experiments for what the tests confirmed. |
| **CFO Dashboard** | Connected ad platforms for spend, a promoted model for contribution and return, a promoted plan for budget and forecast, and experiments for the lift tests. |
| **Creatives**     | A promoted model for creative spend and return.                                                                                    |

When a tile needs a step you haven't taken yet, such as promoting a model or saving a plan, it tells you which step and how many tiles that step unlocks. Some tiles stay hidden until their step is done. Every tile also respects what each person's role can see.

Reports and decks use the same data, and each one includes a section on what the data can't tell you yet.

## Customize a live template for your workspace

**Role:** Workspace Admin

Workspace Admins can change a live template for the whole workspace: rename it, choose its default dates, and pick which sections and tiles it shows.

1. In the template gallery, select **Customise** on a live template.
2. Make your changes:
   - **Template name:** the name shown in the gallery and as the title of dashboards built from it.
   - **Default date range:** **The template's own**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 14 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days**.
   - **Top of the page:** tick the headline tiles to show.
   - **Sections:** use the arrows to change the order, type a new name for a section, turn a section on or off with **Show**, and tick the tiles to show inside it.
3. Select **Save**.

<Image align="center" width="520px" alt="The Customise CMO Dashboard dialog, with the name, default date range, headline tiles and sections" src="https://files.readme.io/d0007b4177c919bc46f488729342d95284a20248f329ad7e1f3667c6c471cb96-12-templates-customise.png" />

Your changes apply to every dashboard built from this template in your workspace, the next time each one opens. Web page, PDF and deck templates can't be customized.

<Callout icon="📘" theme="info">
  **Note:** When Lifesight releases a new version of a template that changes its layout, Workspace Admins see **Update available** on its card. Your dashboards keep the current version until you open the update and select **Accept update**. The update shows what will change, including any of your own changes it would remove. Select **Not now** to keep the current version.
</Callout>

## Frequently asked questions about Templates

### What templates are available?

Eight: three live dashboards (**CMO Dashboard**, **CFO Dashboard** and **Creatives**), two web pages (**Pacing Recommendations** and **Audiences**), two PDF reports (**Ad Channel Performance Report** and **Brand Health**) and one PowerPoint deck (**QBR Deck**).

### Where does a template get its numbers?

From your own workspace: your connected ad platforms, your promoted model, your plans and your experiments.

### Why are some tiles missing or asking me to take a step?

The live templates draw more as your workspace adds a model, a plan and experiments. A tile that needs a step tells you which one. See [What each template needs](#what-each-template-needs).

### Does using a template change the template?

No. **Use template** makes a new dashboard or file from it. Only **Customise** changes the template, and only Workspace Admins can use it.

### Who can customize a template?

Workspace Admins. Everyone else can use templates but not change them.

### Do my changes to a template affect dashboards I've already built?

Yes. Your changes apply to every dashboard built from that template in your workspace, the next time each one opens.

### Can I customize the report and deck templates?

No. **Customise** is for the live dashboard templates. To shape a report or deck, use the template, then ask for changes in the same conversation.

### How long does a template take to build?

A live dashboard can take a minute to open. A report or deck usually takes a few minutes.
