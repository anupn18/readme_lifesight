Logs gives you a full, auditable record of every change Deploy makes to your campaigns, so your team can always trace a budget move back to who made it and why. Whether a change came from a recommendation or from a value you typed yourself, the Logs tab keeps a transparent record of it.

You always know:

* **What** changed, with before and after values.
* **Who** made the change: a workspace member or the system.
* **When** it happened.
* **Why** it happened, linked to the promoted plan and the recommendation it came from.

## Track every applied change

All applied changes are recorded in **Logs**, which you can open from the Deploy header. Each entry includes:

* **Entity:** the campaign or ad set that was updated.
* **Field:** what changed, for example budget, bid target or status.
* **Previous value and new value.**
* **Status:** applied successfully, or flagged with a warning.
* **User:** the workspace member who applied it, or the system.
* **Timestamp:** the exact date and time.

## Find the changes you need

Filter Logs to narrow down to the changes you care about:

* **By user:** see changes applied by you or a teammate.
* **By channel:** focus on a single platform.
* **By plan:** review actions tied to a specific plan.
* **By time:** narrow to a date range.

You can also export Logs for reporting or audit.

## Why logging matters

* **Transparency:** you know exactly how Deploy is shaping your campaigns.
* **Collaboration:** your team can see who applied what.
* **Accountability:** you have an audit trail for finance or leadership review.
* **Confidence:** no adjustment is a black box.

Only Managers and Admins can apply changes. When they do, every action is logged and visible to the whole workspace.

## Frequently Asked Questions<br />

**What does the Logs tab record?**

Every change applied to your campaigns and ad sets through Deploy. Each entry shows the entity, the field that changed, the previous and new values, the status, the user and the timestamp.

**Are custom changes logged, or only recommendations?**

Both. Logs records every applied change, whether it came from a recommendation or from a value you entered yourself.

**Where do I find Logs?**

Open Logs from the Deploy header.

**What kinds of changes are logged?**

Budget, bid target and status changes, along with their before and after values.

**How do I know why a change was made?**

Each entry links back to the promoted plan and the recommendation it came from.

**What does a warning status mean?**

The change was flagged during the push to the ad platform. Review the entry to check whether it applied as expected.

**What does it mean when the user is "system"?**

The change was applied by the system rather than by a workspace member.

**Can I see only my own changes, or a teammate's?**

Yes. Filter by user to see changes applied by you or a specific teammate.

**Can I review changes for one platform or one plan?**

Yes. Filter by channel to focus on a single platform, or by plan to see actions tied to a specific plan.

**Can I look at changes from a specific period?**

Yes. Filter by time to narrow to a date range.

**Can I export Logs?**

Yes. You can export Logs for reporting or audit.

**Who can see Logs?**

Everyone in the workspace. Only Managers and Admins can apply changes, but every logged action is visible to the whole workspace.
