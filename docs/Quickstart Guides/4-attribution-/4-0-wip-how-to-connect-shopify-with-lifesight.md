# How to Connect Shopify with Lifesight

Connect Shopify from the Lifesight 4.0 Integrations workspace so commerce data can be used across supported measurement workflows.

[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Shopify connector in Integrations]

## Before you begin

Confirm that you have permission to install or authorize applications for the Shopify store and permission to manage integrations in Lifesight.

## Connect the store

1. Go to **Integrations** in Lifesight.
2. Find and select **Shopify**.
3. Choose **Connect** or **Add connection**.
4. Enter or select the Shopify store requested by the connector.
5. Sign in to Shopify and review the requested permissions.
6. Approve the connection and return to Lifesight.
7. Confirm that the integration status is active.

[VIDEO PLACEHOLDER: Connecting and authorizing a Shopify store]

## Verify the connection

Review the connection status and latest synchronization information in Integrations. Data availability can take time after the initial authorization.

Keep store identifiers and field definitions stable where possible. If the Shopify store is disconnected or permissions change, reconnect it from Integrations and confirm that synchronization resumes.

> The fields and authentication screens shown during connection can vary based on your Shopify account and the connector configuration enabled for your workspace.
