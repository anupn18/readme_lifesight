**Ask Lifesight is the analyst built into every page of Lifesight.** Ask a question the way you would ask a colleague, such as "Which channels moved most last month, and why?", and Ask Lifesight reads your workspace, runs the analysis, and answers with the numbers, tables, and charts to back it up.

It is not a separate chatbot. Ask Lifesight is one conversation that follows you around the platform. Start a question on the Cockpit, open Attribution, and the same conversation is waiting in the side panel, already aware of what you are looking at.

![An answer from Ask Lifesight in the Cockpit, with a Model insights card that leads with the headline finding](https://files.readme.io/5ccdce7640386687ccf85a88c30a3b3e649166cc53aec50558d9de44699f9a43-ask-hero-cockpit-answer.png)

<Callout icon="📘" theme="info">
  **Every answer is built from your workspace.** Ask Lifesight works from your models, plans, attribution, creatives, and connected ad accounts. Answers tell you which model, plan, or date range they used, and mark any figure Ask Lifesight calculated itself with an asterisk (\*), so you can check the working before you share a number.
</Callout>

## Know what you can ask

You don't need to know where your data lives. Ask the question, and Ask Lifesight picks the right sources.

| Ask about                  | Ask Lifesight works from                                                | Try asking                                                    |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Channel performance**    | Your promoted model                                                     | "Tell me my best performing channels"                         |
| **What changed, and why**  | Causal attribution, plus campaign-level data from your ad platforms     | "Which channels moved most vs the comparison period and why?" |
| **Budget and pacing**      | Your promoted plan and how spend is tracking against it                 | "Is my budget pacing to plan?"                                |
| **Where to move spend**    | Your plan, your model, and how saturated each channel is                | "Where should I reallocate spend to improve ROAS?"            |
| **Forecasts**              | The forecast behind your plan                                           | "Show me the revenue forecast for my plan"                    |
| **Creatives**              | Creative performance and fatigue                                        | "Which creatives should be refreshed and why?"                |
| **Data health**            | The status of your connected integrations                               | "Check the health of all my connected integrations"           |
| **How to use Lifesight**   | The Lifesight documentation                                             | "How do I apply a budget change correctly?"                   |
| **An agent's card**        | The card you asked from                                                 | Select **Ask what went wrong** on a Cockpit card              |

Many answers draw on more than one source. When a budget change is rejected by an ad platform, for example, Ask Lifesight checks the connector's health, reads the change in your plan, and looks up the fix in the Lifesight docs, all in one answer.

## Open Ask Lifesight from anywhere

| Where you are          | How to open it                                                                                       | Best for                                       |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Cockpit**            | Type into the Ask box at the top of the page                                                         | Starting your day and broad questions          |
| **Any other page**     | Select **Ask Lifesight** in the top bar, or press **Alt + C**                                        | Questions about what is on your screen         |
| **A card on the Cockpit** | Select the card's Ask button, such as **Ask what went wrong** or **Ask what breaks downstream**   | Following up on an alert without explaining it |
| **A dashboard**        | Select **Ask Lifesight why** on a tile, or **Ask why** next to the dashboard's summary               | Understanding a number before you act on it    |

On every page other than the Cockpit, Ask Lifesight opens as a side panel next to your work, so you can read the page and the answer side by side. Select **×** to close it. The **Ask Lifesight** button is hidden on the Cockpit, because the whole page is already your Ask space.

## Ask a question

1. Type your question in the Ask box on the Cockpit, or in **Ask anything...** in the side panel.
2. Press **Enter** to send. To start a new line without sending, press **Shift + Enter**.
3. Follow along as Ask Lifesight works. Each step appears as it runs, such as *Reading the attribution report*, *Searching the Lifesight docs*, or *Checking connector health*, with a short summary of its thinking underneath. Select **Hide thinking** to fold the steps away.
4. Read the answer as it streams in.

To stop an answer before it finishes, select the stop button (■) in place of the send button.

![Ask Lifesight showing its thinking and the step it is running while it works on a question](https://files.readme.io/2227c291b83c0066436f9533db026a93a66b9c8bcd5ee93fb6fc82e0c9e20f53-ask-thinking-steps.png)

Answers that need more than one analysis, such as comparing two periods and then finding the campaigns behind the change, can take a minute or two. Longer steps show an estimate next to them, such as "under a minute".

## Ask about what you're looking at

When you ask from the side panel, Ask Lifesight already knows where you are. The line above the box tells you what it is using, such as **Asking about this attribution view** or **Asking about Creative**.

It also uses the settings on the page. Ask "Which channels moved most vs the comparison period and why?" on Attribution, and Ask Lifesight works from the date range, scenario, and level (such as **Channels**) you have selected, then compares them with the period before. You don't have to explain any of it.

To ask about a different period, channel, or scenario, say so in your question, for example "for the last 90 days" or "using Scenario 2".

## Read and check an answer

Answers lead with the finding, then show the evidence.

| Part                          | What it shows                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Headline**                  | The main finding in one line. Answers from an analysis carry its label, such as **Model insights**.                                     |
| **Explanation**               | A short summary and the points behind it, with the key numbers highlighted                                                                      |
| **Tables and charts**         | The evidence, shown in the answer                                                                                                               |
| **Source note**               | The model, plan, or date range the figures come from, such as "All figures come from your champion model..." or **Window & assumptions**       |
| **Asterisk (\*)**             | A figure Ask Lifesight calculated itself, such as the change between two periods, rather than read directly from a Lifesight report or ad platform |
| **More from this analysis**   | Extra tables and charts from the same analysis, folded away until you open them                                                                 |
| **Follow-up questions**       | Suggested next questions. Select one to ask it.                                                                                                 |

![A table in an Ask Lifesight answer, followed by a note naming the model and date range the figures come from](https://files.readme.io/52a5e634669ef5969396c3edcd16dd7bdc5673727e4e2e050417e06526eaa87a-ask-answer-table-source.png)

<Callout icon="📘" theme="info">
  **Check the working before you share a number.** Every answer says where its numbers come from. When a figure is marked with an asterisk (\*), the end of the answer lists how it was worked out, under a line such as "Derived by Ask Lifesight, not read from the platform (\*)".
</Callout>

### Expand, download, and inspect a chart or table

Select the expand icon on any chart or table in an answer to open it in a larger view.

| Control          | What it does                                     |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **View**         | Shows the chart. Hover over it to see values.    |
| **Data**         | Shows the numbers behind the chart as a table    |
| **Download PNG** | Saves the chart as an image                      |
| **Download CSV** | Saves the numbers as a CSV file                  |
| **Copy link**    | Copies a link to this chart                      |

## Rate answers and keep the conversation going

Under every answer you will find:

* **Follow-up questions:** suggested next steps, such as "Drill into Facebook campaigns to see what drove the conversion drop". Select one to ask it.
* **Read aloud:** plays the answer as audio.
* **Helpful** (👍) and **Not helpful** (👎): tell us how the answer landed.

When you select **Not helpful**, a box asks "What was off? A wrong figure, the wrong model, missing detail..." Add a note and select **Send**, or select **Skip the note**. Your ratings and notes go to the Lifesight team and help improve future answers.

![Follow-up questions under an answer, and the note box that opens after selecting Not helpful](https://files.readme.io/2d867f3754490e88d777e76e87380ec7ad0633e753b386fb1f22d58e9b3cad54-ask-followups-feedback.png)

## Pick up where you left off

Every conversation is saved and titled for you from your first question, so you can come back to it any time.

| Where                    | What you can do                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cockpit**              | Your three most recent conversations sit under **Pick up where you left off**. Select one to reopen it. Select the history icon in the Ask box to search **All conversations** by title.            |
| **A conversation on the Cockpit** | **Home** takes you back to the Cockpit and keeps your conversation. **New thread** starts fresh. **History** opens your **Conversations** list. Hover over a conversation and select **⋯** to **Rename** or **Archive** it. |
| **Side panel**           | Select the history icon to search your conversations or pick from **Recents**. Select the new chat icon to start fresh.                                                                             |

## Get better answers

| You want                          | Ask                                                                          |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A quick read on performance       | "Tell me my best performing channels"                                        |
| To understand a change            | "Which channels moved most vs the previous 30 days, and why?"                |
| A budget decision                 | "Where should I reallocate spend to improve ROAS next month?"                |
| To check on your plan             | "Is my budget pacing to plan this week? Which channels are off pace?"        |

* **Be specific.** Name the metric, the period, and the level you care about. "Facebook campaigns over the last 14 days, by iROAS" gets a sharper answer than "How is Facebook doing?"
* **Ask from the right page.** Open the page that shows what you mean, then ask from the side panel, and Ask Lifesight starts from that view.
* **Keep going in the same conversation.** Ask Lifesight remembers what you have discussed, so a follow-up like "Now break that down by campaign" just works.

## Fix common issues

**The answer is taking a while.**
Questions that need more than one analysis take longer. Ask Lifesight shows each step as it runs, so you can see what it is doing. To stop, select the stop button (■).

**The answer used a different date range than I expected.**
Answers state the dates they used, in the source note or under **Window & assumptions**. From the side panel, Ask Lifesight starts from the dates on your page. Ask again with the dates you want, for example "for 1 to 30 November".

**A number in the answer has an asterisk.**
Ask Lifesight calculated that figure itself, for example the change between two periods, rather than reading it directly from a report. The end of the answer shows how it was worked out.

**I can't find the Ask Lifesight button on the Cockpit.**
It is hidden on the Cockpit, because the whole page is your Ask space. Use the Ask box at the top of the page.

**I can't find an old conversation.**
Open History and search by title. Titles are generated from your first question, so search for the topic rather than your exact words.

**An answer looks wrong.**
Select **Not helpful** and tell us what was off, such as a wrong figure, the wrong model, or missing detail. Then ask again with more detail, for example the date range or model you expected.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Ask Lifesight?**
Ask Lifesight is the AI analyst built into the Lifesight platform. You ask questions about your marketing in plain English, and it answers with numbers, tables, and charts built from your own workspace data, including your models, plans, attribution, creatives, and connected ad accounts.

**Where do I find Ask Lifesight?**
On the Cockpit, use the Ask box at the top of the page. On any other page, select **Ask Lifesight** in the top bar or press **Alt + C** to open it as a side panel. You can also select the Ask button on any Cockpit card, such as **Ask what went wrong**, or **Ask Lifesight why** on a dashboard tile.

**What questions can I ask Ask Lifesight?**
You can ask about channel performance and incremental revenue, what changed between two periods and why, budget pacing against your plan, where to reallocate spend, forecasts, creative performance, the health of your data connections, and how to use Lifesight.

**Where does Ask Lifesight get its numbers?**
From your Lifesight workspace: your promoted model, causal attribution, plans and forecasts, creative data, and the campaign data from your connected ad platforms. For questions about how to use Lifesight, it searches the Lifesight documentation. Each answer names the model, plan, or date range it used.

**What does an asterisk (\*) next to a number mean in Ask Lifesight?**
It marks a figure Ask Lifesight calculated itself, such as the change between two periods, rather than reading it directly from a Lifesight report or ad platform. The end of the answer explains how each marked figure was worked out.

**Does Ask Lifesight know which page I'm on?**
Yes. When you ask from the side panel, Ask Lifesight uses the page you are on and its settings, such as the date range, scenario, and level on Attribution. The line above the box, such as "Asking about this attribution view", shows what it is using.

**Does my conversation stay open when I move to another page?**
Yes. Ask Lifesight is one conversation that follows you around the platform. Start on the Cockpit, move to Attribution, and the same conversation is waiting in the side panel.

**Why does Ask Lifesight take time to answer some questions?**
Some questions need more than one analysis, such as comparing two periods and then finding the campaigns that drove the change. Ask Lifesight shows each step as it runs, and you can stop an answer at any time with the stop button.

**Can I see how Ask Lifesight reached its answer?**
Yes. While it works, Ask Lifesight shows each step it runs and a summary of its thinking. Every answer also states its sources and date range, and you can expand any chart or table and open its **Data** tab to see the numbers behind it.

**Can I download a chart or table from an answer?**
Yes. Select the expand icon on the chart or table, then select **Download PNG** for an image or **Download CSV** for the data. **Copy link** copies a link to the chart.

**How do I give feedback on an Ask Lifesight answer?**
Select **Helpful** or **Not helpful** under the answer. After **Not helpful**, you can add a note about what was off, such as a wrong figure or the wrong model, and select **Send**, or select **Skip the note**.

**How do I find, rename, or archive a past conversation?**
Select the history icon in the Ask box or the side panel to search your conversations by title. In a conversation on the Cockpit, select **History**, hover over a conversation, and select **⋯** to **Rename** or **Archive** it.

**Are Ask Lifesight's suggestions the same for everyone?**
No. The suggested questions on the Cockpit are chosen for your persona, and the starters in the side panel change with the page you are on. You can change your persona in your profile.
