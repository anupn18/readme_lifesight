Conversion APIs enable real-time tracking and reporting of customer actions across your digital platforms. By directly capturing conversion events server-side, you'll get more accurate data, improve attribution, and enhance your ability to optimize campaigns—even in the face of ad-blocking tools or browser restrictions. This powerful feature helps you make data-driven decisions and maximize your marketing ROI with greater confidence.

## What is Data Activation?

Lifesight’s data activation stack combines best-in-class data collection, enrichment and activation features into a simple no-code first-party data management tool that helps marketers fully leverage their data in the privacy-first era. Our data activation stack uses client and server-side tracking along with our first-party identity graph technology to ensure highest quality data is collected and enriched before sending it off to downstream platforms.

Using traditional client side tracking only leads to signal loss due to browser limitations introduced by privacy-first changes. Server-side tracking solves this issue by sending all possible conversion events using an API. Loss of signal reduces the ability for ad channels to perform to its full potential.

## Pain points without CAPI setup

* Inability to build complete retargeting lists
* Inability to use AI to target ads to new customers
* Inability to accurately attribute conversions to generate in-platform reports
* Inability to deliver dynamic product ads effectively
* High Cost Per Conversion
* High CPM
* Low ROAS
* Unable to scale their ad channel

## Features

* Server-side tracking: Send 100% conversion events to ad channels.
* Identity resolution/ID Graph
* Event enrichment
* Enable/Disable the events you want to send

## Benefits

* Set up server-side tracking of CAPI in minutes
* Only send conversions that matter to you
* Improved ad channel performance
* Improved in-platform attribution reporting
* Lower Cost Per Conversion
* Increased ROAS
* Lower CPM
