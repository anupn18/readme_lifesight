Analyze gives every stakeholder the same causal read—raw vs incrementality‑adjusted KPIs, realized vs forecast with p50/p10/p90 bands, and an evidence trail—so marketing, finance, and channel teams make decisions from one truth.

At a glance

Purpose: Replace siloed dashboards with a governed system of decision‑ready views that reconcile planning, testing, and daily operations.

Who it’s for: CMO/VP Growth, CFO & FP&A, Performance & Media, Analytics & Insights, Agency Partners.

Decisions you’ll make:

Are we on plan (profit/revenue/CAC) and within risk bands?

Which channels/tactics are incremental and where is saturation emerging?

What changed—tests, calibration, or guardrails—and who approved it?

Primary outputs:

Executive, Finance, and Ops dashboards built on incremental truth

Realized vs Forecast with uncertainty bands and variance explanations

Raw vs Adjusted (incrementality‑calibrated) KPIs side‑by‑side

Evidence & Versioning: model/test anchors, calibration log, policy changes

What Analyze includes

Executive Summary

p50/p10/p90 outcomes vs realized (Profit/Revenue/CAC/Payback)

“This week’s decisions” snapshot (scale/cap/maintain) linked to Optimize

How Analyze works (high level)

Causal backbone: Pulls contribution and marginal curves from Measure (MMM), anchors them with geo‑test lift, and reconciles daily KPIs via incrementality‑calibrated attribution.

Planning context: Loads the Approved Plan from Forecast with uncertainty bands for realized vs forecast tracking.

Governance: Surfaces versioning (models, tests, policies) so every number is traceable to data and decisions.

Typical workflows
Weekly business review (CMO + CFO + Channel leads)

Open Executive Summary → Are we inside bands?

Check variance drivers → promo, supply, mix, auction shifts.

Review incremental contribution and marginal headroom → agree on Scale/Cap/Test priorities.

Log decisions; hand off to Optimize for execution.

Monthly calibration & re‑plan sync

Compare raw vs adjusted deltas; incorporate new geo tests; confirm MMM refresh status.

If reality diverges from p10/p90 or assumptions changed, trigger a scenario update in Forecast.

Campaign/Channel owner daily ops

Work from adjusted KPIs; monitor guardrail adherence (caps, floors, ramp‑rates, frequency).

Use Explore & Compare to isolate tactic or cohort performance; save views for repeat checks.

Executive/Board prep

Export one‑page Finance Pack: p50/p10/p90, realized vs forecast, key moves and risk notes, evidence log summary.

Interpreting key views

Raw vs Adjusted KPIs: Raw shows platform perspective; Adjusted reflects causal truth. Expect retargeting/brand search to drop and proven prospecting/upper‑funnel to rise.

Contribution & Curves: Rank channels by incremental share; use curves to see where dollars become waste (flat mROAS).

Bands, not points: Hitting p50 is ideal; staying above p10 is resilience. Two consecutive p10 breaches → re‑plan.

Evidence Log: Treat it as your audit trail—what evidence changed the numbers and when.

Decision recipes (ready to use)

Scale channels sitting on steep mROAS with headroom; cap those at or beyond saturation.

Rebalance from over‑credited harvesters (retargeting/brand) to proven prospecting (YouTube/CTV/Social) using adjusted KPIs.

Trigger a re‑plan if: p10 breached two weeks in a row, a decisive test lands, or promo/inventory assumptions change materially.

Schedule a test when uncertainty bands are wide on a high‑spend channel.

Data requirements & mapping

Minimum viable

Weekly outcomes (orders/revenue/subscriptions) and channel spend

Seasonality/promo calendar

Latest Measure run and the current Approved Plan

Better

Geo splits, POS/retail roll‑ups, new vs returning flags

Standard splits: Brand vs Non‑Brand (Search), Prospecting vs Retargeting (Social/Programmatic)

Creative/audience tags; frequency distribution for video

Best

Recent geo‑test readouts; MMM version(s) with anchor notes

Incrementality‑adjusted daily KPIs; inventory/distribution flags; macro/competitive proxies

Mapping rules

One timezone, one currency, aligned ISO weeks

Total‑to‑truth dedupe: adjusted totals never exceed actual outcomes

Window standards (e.g., 7‑day click, 1‑day view) documented and consistent

Governance, refresh & versioning

Refresh cadence: Weekly ops, monthly calibration, quarterly MMM refresh and plan approval.

Versioning: Each dashboard view links to model/test/policy versions; every change is timestamped with owner and rationale.

Permissions: Role‑based access (Exec, Finance, Ops, Analyst); sensitive notes (e.g., margin) scoped to need‑to‑know.

Troubleshooting

Numbers don’t match platform: Check raw vs adjusted toggle and attribution windows; confirm calibration version.

Unexpected channel drop/rise: Look at the Evidence Log (new test or calibration), then review curves/uncertainty.

Variance unexplained: Ensure promo/price and inventory flags are present; annotate external shocks.

Conflicting cohort/geo stories: Verify taxonomy (Brand/Non‑Brand; Prospecting/Retargeting) and geo mapping consistency.

FAQs

Why show raw and adjusted together?
Transparency. Teams see platform context while optimizing to incremental truth.

Can we customize dashboards?
Yes—save filters and layouts (time/geo/channel/cohort); share stable links for recurring reviews and support.

How does Analyze relate to Optimize and Forecast?
Analyze diagnoses and aligns; Optimize acts with guardrails; Forecast plans with risk bands. All draw from the same causal evidence.

Will Analyze work without geo tests?
Yes, using MMM. But decisive tests tighten uncertainty and improve trust—run them where stakes are high.
