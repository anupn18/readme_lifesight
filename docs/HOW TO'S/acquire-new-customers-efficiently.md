Acquiring new customers efficiently means funding incremental demand creation—not just demand capture—so you grow the new‑to‑brand base at or below your CAC/payback targets. The playbook below shows how to diagnose today’s mix, prove lift for prospecting channels, set guardrails that prevent over‑spend on harvesters, and operate with incrementality‑adjusted KPIs.

At a glance

Use when: New‑customer growth has slowed, CAC has crept up, spend is concentrated in retargeting/brand search, or you’re adding CTV/YouTube/TikTok/Influencer.

You’ll achieve: A funded prospecting plan with proven lift, calibrated iCAC/iROAS, channel guardrails (caps/floors), and a paced budget that meets payback at p50 and remains acceptable at p10.

Core tools: Geo‑based Incrementality Testing · Causal MMM · Incrementality‑calibrated Attribution · Ensemble Forecasting & Scenario Planner.

Audience, goal, and outcomes

Audience: CMO/VP Growth, CFO & FP&A, Performance & Media, Analytics & Insights
Goal: Increase new‑to‑brand customers at or below target CAC/payback by rebalancing spend from over‑credited harvesters to proven incremental prospecting with headroom.
Timeframe: 4–8 weeks (includes test run time); quick wins in week 1 with baseline & calibration.
Outcomes:

Causal baseline: incremental vs baseline, marginal returns and saturation

Prospecting lift proven via geo tests; attribution calibrated to incrementality

Approved reallocation plan with caps/floors and prospecting floors

Weekly pacing worksheet; monitoring cadence; next‑test agenda

What “Acquire efficiently” means (clear definitions)

New customer (NTB): First purchase within an agreed historical window (e.g., 12–24 months); flag via commerce/CRM, not cookies.

Efficient: Meets CAC or payback targets on an incremental basis (iCAC, not raw CPA).

Prospecting vs Harvesting:

Prospecting (YouTube/CTV/Upper‑funnel Social/Non‑Brand discovery) creates new demand.

Harvesting (Retargeting, Brand Search) captures demand that often would convert anyway—cap it.

Step‑by‑step plan

1. Diagnose current mix & baseline (Days 1–3)

Run Measure to decompose baseline vs media and view marginal returns (mROAS) by channel/tactic.

Split the taxonomy Brand vs Non‑Brand (Search) and Prospecting vs Retargeting (Social/Programmatic).

Create a draft scale / cap / test list with special attention to Retargeting and Brand Search saturation.

2. Instrument “new customer” correctly (Days 1–5, in parallel)

Ensure your outcome feed has a new_vs_returning flag and consistent customer keys (email hash or account ID).

For omnichannel, include POS roll‑ups so retail NTB impact is counted.

Standardize lookbacks and one primary KPI: New Orders or New Customer Revenue (plus total Revenue for halo).

3. Calibrate daily KPIs to incrementality (Days 4–7)

Apply incrementality‑calibrated attribution so dashboards show iCAC / iROAS and NTB share by channel/tactic.

Expect adjusted credit to decrease for harvesters and increase for proven prospecting (CTV/YouTube/Prospecting Social).

4. Prove lift for prospecting bets (Weeks 2–6)

Launch geo scale‑ups for YouTube/CTV/Prospecting Social or Non‑Brand clusters with high upside; target ≥80% power.

Run retargeting/brand search caps as holdout tests in matched geos to quantify over‑credit.

Feed lift into MMM and attribution the moment read‑outs land.

5. Reallocate with payback discipline (Days 7–12)

In the planner, build Efficiency, Balanced, and Growth scenarios.

Choose one objective (Profit, Revenue, or Payback/CAC) and keep it constant across scenarios.

Approve the plan that meets the objective at p50 and remains acceptable at p10.

Set prospecting floors and harvester caps (see Guardrails).

6. Operate week‑to‑week on incremental KPIs (Weeks 2–8)

Shift marginal dollars toward channels with steep mROAS and headroom.

Use creative & audience refreshes to open new reach; enforce frequency discipline in upper‑funnel video.

Keep a variance log (auction shifts, promos, supply) and act via pre‑agreed rules.

7. Close the loop (ongoing)

Weekly: Realized vs forecast (NTB volume, iCAC, payback).

Monthly: Calibration sweep; minor re‑plans; new test launches for high‑$ uncertainties.

Quarterly: MMM refresh; rerun scenarios; revisit floors/caps.

Channel‑level playbook (where efficient acquisition usually comes from)

Paid Search

Non‑Brand Discovery: Expand incremental query clusters; protect with negatives to avoid Brand cannibalization.

Brand Search: Cap impression share and exact match after a threshold; reinvest savings into Non‑Brand + upper‑funnel.

Paid Social / Programmatic

Prospecting: Broad or modeled audiences for reach; creative diversity (story, benefit, offer) to avoid fatigue; enforce frequency bands (e.g., F1–2 vs F3–5) and cap high‑freq cohorts.

Retargeting: Tighten recency windows; cap spend as % of total; treat as harvest support, not growth engine.

YouTube / CTV

Tiered geo scale‑ups; measure halo into Search/Direct; optimize reach & frequency, not only click metrics.

Influencer / Affiliate

Favor creators with genuine reach in new audiences; watch coupon/last‑click gaming; evaluate NTB% and iCAC, not vanity engagement.

Retail Media (if applicable)

Shift beyond branded terms to category and competitive discovery; measure halo into e‑comm and POS.

Creative & offer strategy (levers that unlock efficient NTB)

Message‑market fit: Concept pillars across Problem → Promise → Proof → Price/Offer.

Formats that scale: Short video for reach (YouTube/CTV/TikTok), then derivatives for social placements; refresh cadence every 2–4 weeks.

Landing & onboarding: Friction‑lite flows, clear first‑order value, starter offers that preserve payback (avoid promo addiction).

Testing approach: Use geo multi‑cell designs or controlled holdouts for major creative packages; reserve in‑platform split tests for micro‑iterations.

Guardrails & operating rules (copy, then tailor)

Prospecting floor: ≥ X% of paid media (or ≥ Y% of weekly new orders).

Retargeting cap: ≤ A% of total paid spend or ≤ B% share of conversions post‑calibration.

Brand Search cap: Impression share or spend cap tied to exact‑match opportunity; strict negatives for cannibalization.

Frequency discipline (upper‑funnel): Cap high‑freq cohorts; reinvest in reach bands F1–2.

Ramp rates: ± 20% WoW limit per channel unless pre‑approved.

Channel concentration: ≤ 40% spend in a single channel without CFO sign‑off.

Promo sync: Align media weight with price/promo calendar; treat promo impact as baseline in measurement.

KPIs to run and report (incremental, not raw)

iCAC (incremental CAC) and iROAS by channel/tactic

NTB volume and NTB share of total orders/revenue

Payback (weeks or months) at p50 and p10

Prospecting share of spend and Prospecting:Retargeting ratio

mROAS‑weighted budget shift (marginal dollars flow to steep curves)

Creative reach & frequency mix (F1–2 vs high‑freq exposure)

Data requirements (good → better → best)

Good: Orders/revenue with new_vs_returning flag; spend by channel/tactic; seasonality/promo calendar.

Better: Geo splits; brand vs non‑brand and prospecting vs retargeting labels; creative/audience tags.

Best: POS roll‑ups (if omnichannel); recent geo test results for prospecting; macro/competitive proxies (share of search, category interest).

CFO & FP&A alignment checklist

Shared definitions for new customer, contribution margin, returns/COGS, and payback.

Visibility of raw vs incrementality‑adjusted metrics and the evidence log behind adjustments.

Plan chosen at p50 with acceptable p10 downside; re‑plan triggers documented.

Clear explanation of harvester caps and prospecting floors tied to lift and marginal curves.

Common pitfalls (and how to avoid them)

Over‑relying on harvesters → Cap Retargeting/Brand Search; reinvest into proven prospecting.

Optimizing to average ROAS → Use marginal curves; avoid flat regions.

Counting promos as “acquisition” → Treat promo impact as baseline; don’t over‑credit media.

Weak NTB identification → Fix customer key and lookback; avoid cookie‑based heuristics.

Under‑powered tests → Increase duration, geo pairs, or spend delta; avoid asymmetric calendars.

Creative fatigue → Refresh concepts regularly; diversify placements and lengths.

Taxonomy drift → Enforce Brand/Non‑Brand and Prospecting/Retargeting upstream.

30•60•90 plan (sample)

Days 1–30: Baseline Measure, taxonomy/windows hygiene, calibration of KPIs, first reallocation with prospecting floor and harvester caps.

Days 31–60: Run prospecting scale‑up geo tests (YouTube/CTV/Social); refresh calibration; expand NTB‑positive Non‑Brand clusters.

Days 61–90: MMM refresh; new plan approval; creative pillar refresh; add second‑order tests (offer variants, frequency bands).
