## Weekly/monthly Lifesight checklist:

* Check the [Goals](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/goals) tab to make sure you are on the right track to meeting your goals. Everytime your model refreshes you get the latest probability of hitting your goals and weekly actual vs predicted spends and outcomes.
* View the [Recommendations](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/recommendations) tab to get budget reallocation suggestions for channels and tactics. By applying these recommendations you would see results as predicted by the model.
* Visit the MMM [Model tab](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/model) to check how your model accuracy has changed after the data refresh.  A low forecast error means your model has good prediction accuracy. 
* Generally, a high R^2 value, low NRMSE value and low Estimation error % indicates that a model is still accurate and not affected by model drift. Learn more about how to: [validate your results.](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/validate-your-results)
