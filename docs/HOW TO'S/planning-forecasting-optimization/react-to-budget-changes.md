Budget allocation plays a critical role in determining the efficiency and impact of marketing campaigns. With Lifesight, marketing teams can assess how changes in budget affect key performance metrics and optimize their strategy accordingly.

## Facing a budget cut and need to replan your investments

1. Run a MMM model to understand how your current investments influence marketing performance.
2. Use the MMM [Scenario Planner](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/budget-optimizer-1) and select Optimize for "Budget".
3. Enter your new budget amount in the Target field. 
4. Select your forecast period (either month or quarter) and set your [constraints](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/compare-plans) according to the budget changes for the model to allocate media spends.
5. Click `Run Scenario` to view the forecast and media spend allocation.
6. The model predicts and allocates the reduced budget amongst your media mix while optimizing for your outcome KPI (Revenue, etc)
7. The [Planner](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/how-to-build-a-plan) also gives a budget worksheet and pacing sheet that can be downloaded and shared with channel managers to implement.

### For example,

If your previous month budget was $100,000 and you want to optimize your budget for next month and have only $70,000. Then you would input select `Optimize` for "Budget" and enter `Target Budget` as $70,000. You would then select `Forecast` for "Next month" and choose `Constraints` as "Aggressive". This indicates that you the model has to allocate only 70% of the budget to drive maximum business outcomes. Click `Run Scenario` to view your new budget allocation and predicted outcomes.
