Give every person on your team access to exactly what they need, so they can do their job in Lifesight without seeing or changing more than they should.&#x20;

Each person is assigned one role, and that role decides which modules they can open and which actions they can take. Lifesight includes six preset roles, each with **Read** access (view results) or **Manage** access (create, edit, and act) to the modules it covers. When someone opens a module outside their role, they see an **Access Restricted** screen where they can request access from a Workspace Admin.

When the preset roles don't match how your team works, **Custom Roles** let you tailor access to specific modules and platform capabilities. For example, you can give an agency partner read-only access to Attribution while keeping Planner and Settings limited to your internal team.
