Geo-experiments require careful selection of **experimental units**, or **geos**, to ensure accurate, localized measurement. This article explains how to structure geo-experiments in the United States, focusing on **Designated Market Areas (DMAs)** and other geo-set options. LifeSight provides support in choosing the right geo granularity for various countries to maximize precision and relevance.

## Identifying Geos

In geo-experiments, the market is divided into **geos** to serve as test and control units. This segmentation allows you to apply interventions regionally and measure their impact in a targeted way.

### Key Terminology

* **Geosets**: The level of **granularity** chosen to divide the market (e.g., states, DMAs, or counties), which affects the precision and feasibility of the experiment.
* **Geos**: The individual units within a geoset. For example, if states are selected as the geoset, each state becomes a geo. DMAs or counties provide even finer granularity.

### Geo-Set Levels for the United States

* **State-Level**: Each state is a geo, providing broad segmentation for high-level analysis.
* **County-Level**: Counties offer detailed, local-level analysis but increase data collection complexity.
* **DMA-Level**: **Designated Market Areas (DMAs)** align with media markets (e.g., TV or radio) and strike a balance between state and county levels, making them ideal for ad-focused experiments.

## Benefits of Using DMAs in Geo-Experiments

DMAs provide a **middle-ground** for U.S.-based geo-experiments, especially for media-related interventions. By defining regions based on shared media access, DMAs enable:

* **Targeted Media Delivery**: Ads can be precisely focused within individual DMAs, aligning with media consumption patterns.
* **Localized Insights**: Different DMAs respond uniquely, allowing for nuanced analysis.
* **Reduced Spillover**: DMAs minimize overlap with adjacent markets, enhancing experimental clarity.

## Key Requirements for Successful Geo-Experiments

To achieve reliable results, follow these best practices:

1. **Target Ads by Geo**  
   * Ensure ads are delivered only within selected geos (states, DMAs, etc.) to maintain distinct test and control groups.

2. **Track Local Metrics**  
   * Measure outcomes like conversions and sales within each geo to capture regional effects.

3. **Minimize Spillover**  
   * Choose geos with natural boundaries (such as DMAs) to avoid spillover effects where treatment influences neighboring areas.

## Choosing the Right Geo Granularity

Selecting the appropriate geo level depends on your objectives, data availability, and intervention type:

* **State-Level**: Best for broad, high-level insights when fine granularity isn’t necessary.
* **DMA-Level**: Ideal for media-centric interventions, as DMAs align with natural media markets.
* **County-Level**: Suitable for highly detailed analysis, though it can be complex and resource-intensive at scale.
