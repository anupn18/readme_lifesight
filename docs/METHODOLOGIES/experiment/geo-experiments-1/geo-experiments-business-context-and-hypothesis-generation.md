When embarking on an experiment, the foundational step is to establish a **clear hypothesis** rooted in the business context. This hypothesis defines the purpose of the experiment by outlining the expected outcome based on specific **business objectives and current challenges**. A well-crafted hypothesis aligns with key strategic questions, such as identifying the incremental value of a new channel. By starting with a business-aligned hypothesis, you ensure that the experiment addresses meaningful questions, paving the way for actionable insights and more effective decision-making.

### How LifeSight Can Help ?

LifeSight leverages a clever combination of **scale-up and holdout testing methods** to help you assess each hypothesis effectively. Depending on the objective, experiments can be designed to scale up a channel’s investment or hold out from certain audiences to isolate its true impact. These approaches allow you to measure incremental effects with precision, ensuring that your insights are based on observable changes rather than assumptions.

Using insights from your MMM model, LifeSight helps generate hypotheses for channels that need closer analysis. Our marketing science team provides support in testing and validating hypotheses tailored to your business context. The following LifeSight Experimentation Table outlines key testing areas and LifeSight’s role in enabling meaningful experimentation.
