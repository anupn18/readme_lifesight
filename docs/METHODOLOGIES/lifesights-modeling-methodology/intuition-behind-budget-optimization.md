You can find the details around how to use Lifesight's Budget Optimiser [here](https://docs.lifesight.io/docs/budget-optimizer-1) 

Here we try to explain how the budget optimizer works under the hood.

Budget optimizer relies on 4 key things to generate its recommendations : Constraints, mROAS (or mCPA), Period of optimization & Slope of the saturation curve
