**Average Solution Method** is an approach for optimizing model selection, balancing both **model fit** and **business fit** to improve decision-making.

The method assigns weights to key parameters based on their impact on overall model performance. These parameters may include performance metrics like NRMSE and channel contribution metrics. After filtering for business relevance, models are evaluated, and the **top 30 solutions (solIDs)** are selected for further analysis. This ensures that the final models optimize both statistical accuracy and business outcomes, such as ROAS (Return on Ad Spend).
